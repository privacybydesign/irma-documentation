## What this document is

The document "enumerates obligations and procedures of the System Owner & Top-Level Scheme Owner in relation to the NL EUDI Wallet certification system" ([§1, p. 5](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-03_Org_Tech_Proc_System_Owner_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_pub.pdf#page=5)). It also describes the role of the Supervisory Body in surveillance of the Trust Framework, "given its strong influence on risks to the EUDI Wallet related services which are subject to certification" ([§1, p. 5](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-03_Org_Tech_Proc_System_Owner_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_pub.pdf#page=5)). Its current scope "covers supervising the application and maintenance obligations for the the certification (schemes) for Wallet Providers and PID Providers ()" ([§1, p. 5](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-03_Org_Tech_Proc_System_Owner_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_pub.pdf#page=5)).

## Scope and object of certification

- For the Netherlands, the System Owner & Top-Level Scheme Owner will be the Minister of Economic Affairs ([§1, p. 5](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-03_Org_Tech_Proc_System_Owner_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_pub.pdf#page=5)).
- The document covers governance and maintenance, technical vulnerability management, marks and labels, the Trust Framework (supervision of CABs and of wallets, access and registration certificates, registration of Wallet Providers), reporting and notification to the European Commission, complaint handling and secured exchange of information ([§Table of Content, p. 4](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-03_Org_Tech_Proc_System_Owner_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_pub.pdf#page=4)).
- It does not define an object of certification; the certification system is established for compliance with Regulation (EU) 2024/1183 amending Regulation (EU) No 910/2014 on the basis of ISO/IEC 17067 and ISO/IEC 17065 and operated under the accreditation system of Regulation 765/2008 ([§2, p. 6](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-03_Org_Tech_Proc_System_Owner_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_pub.pdf#page=6)).

## Who it applies to

assessing changes and updating governance, documentation and procedures; integrating information from certification, notification, monitoring and statistical reporting; operating a periodic review procedure ([§3.2, p. 7](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-03_Org_Tech_Proc_System_Owner_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_pub.pdf#page=7)); transmitting draft national schemes to the European Digital Identity Cooperation Group ([§3.3.1, p. 8](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-03_Org_Tech_Proc_System_Owner_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_pub.pdf#page=8)); discussing the system and top-level scheme periodically with peers in the EUDIW Certification System Collaboration Group when relevant changes or events occur ([§3.3.2, p. 8](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-03_Org_Tech_Proc_System_Owner_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_pub.pdf#page=8)); ## Key points

Organisation and governance
- System and Top-level Scheme Owner: Minister of Economic Affairs, in close collaboration with the Minister of the Interior ([§3.1, p. 7](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-03_Org_Tech_Proc_System_Owner_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_pub.pdf#page=7)).
- Supported by the "NL EUDIW Certification System Management Board" ([§3.1, p. 7](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-03_Org_Tech_Proc_System_Owner_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_pub.pdf#page=7)).
- Reviews are both ad hoc (triggered by significant technical or regulatory updates) and periodic ([§3.2, p. 7](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-03_Org_Tech_Proc_System_Owner_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_pub.pdf#page=7)).
- Changes resulting from security incidents or vulnerabilities are handled per Chapter 4 ([§3.2, p. 7](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-03_Org_Tech_Proc_System_Owner_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_pub.pdf#page=7)).
- Under art. 5c(4) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1183, the System Owner transmits the draft national certification schemes to the European Digital Identity Cooperation Group, which may issue opinions and recommendations ([§3.3.1, p. 8](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-03_Org_Tech_Proc_System_Owner_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_pub.pdf#page=8)).
- The EUDIW Certification System Collaboration Group's "end-objective" is "to ensure a harmonized high level of assurance of the European Digital Identity Wallet throughout Europe"; it also targets "a smooth transition towards harmonized EUDIW European Certification Schemes" ([§3.3.2, p. 8](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-03_Org_Tech_Proc_System_Owner_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_pub.pdf#page=8)).

Vulnerability management
- The procedure relates to the [NL-P-P](/edi-stelsel/documents/nl-p-p)/W series (resilience, software development and vulnerability management requirements) and is established in accordance with CIR (EU) 2024/2981 and the NIS 2 Directive ([§4.1, p. 9](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-03_Org_Tech_Proc_System_Owner_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_pub.pdf#page=9)).
- A CAB-CB suspension decision "is to be cascaded up to the top-level certificate" and the NL EUDIW top-level Certificate Scheme Owner notifies the European Commission ([§4.1, p. 10](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-03_Org_Tech_Proc_System_Owner_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_pub.pdf#page=10)).
- "It is upon the CAB decision to prolong the period of certificate suspension, if it is justified" ([§4.1, p. 10](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-03_Org_Tech_Proc_System_Owner_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_pub.pdf#page=10)).
- For components certified under EUCC, CIR 2024/482, chapter VI "Vulnerability Management and Vulnerability Disclosure" applies and the Scheme Owner cooperates with the National Cybersecurity Certification Authority ([§4.1, p. 10](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-03_Org_Tech_Proc_System_Owner_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_pub.pdf#page=10)).

Marks
- The EUDI Wallet Trust Mark is related to the top-level certificate issued according to the procedure in [NL-TL](/edi-stelsel/documents/nl-tl) ([§5.1, p. 11](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-03_Org_Tech_Proc_System_Owner_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_pub.pdf#page=11)).
- The Wallet Instance of the certified Wallet Solution "shall incorporate a dedicated Trust Mark UI view" that renders the official Trust Mark graphics/logo, displays localised informational text on the certification, and links to a list of certified Wallet Solutions and to a page with the certification information of the user's active Wallet Solution ([§5.1, p. 11](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-03_Org_Tech_Proc_System_Owner_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_pub.pdf#page=11)).
- The NL EUDI Certification System "shall establish an official mark of conformity" attesting successful evaluation and certification of relevant elements of the Wallet solution ([§5.2, p. 11](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-03_Org_Tech_Proc_System_Owner_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_pub.pdf#page=11)).
- The mark is presented under the brand "NL EUDI Wallet certification system"; graphical rules are in a dedicated publication by the Minister of Economic Affairs; the logo is complemented by a QR code with a URL to a portal hosted by the Certification System Owner ([§5.3, p. 11-12](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-03_Org_Tech_Proc_System_Owner_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_pub.pdf#page=11)).
- The System Owner keeps a register of entities entitled to use the mark, the associated certificates and their status (current, suspended, revoked or expired) ([§5.2, p. 11](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-03_Org_Tech_Proc_System_Owner_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_pub.pdf#page=11)).
- Permitted uses: system/scheme information issued by the System/Scheme Owner; official CAB or Scheme Owner documents; information platforms linked to the certified product or service provider with clear indication of the certification; technical and commercial documentation produced by the certificate holder ([§5.4, p. 12](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-03_Org_Tech_Proc_System_Owner_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_pub.pdf#page=12)). Other uses require prior approval of the Scheme Owner ([§5.4, p. 12](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-03_Org_Tech_Proc_System_Owner_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_pub.pdf#page=12)).
- Prohibited: use on products, services or versions not included in the certificate; modifying or combining the mark with other trademarks or logos; use in relation to suspended, revoked or expired certificates; improper use "will be subject to legal action" ([§5.4, p. 12](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-03_Org_Tech_Proc_System_Owner_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_pub.pdf#page=12)).

Trust Framework and supervision
- The Supervisory Body oversees CABs through ex-ante and ex-post supervision, monitors their competence, impartiality, independence and integrity, reviews certification processes and decisions, conducts on-site and off-site supervision, may demand documentation, investigates complaints and irregularities, may require corrective actions, may order suspension of certification activities and require review, suspension or withdrawal of affected certificates, and prepares periodic reports ([§6.1, p. 13-14](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-03_Org_Tech_Proc_System_Owner_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_pub.pdf#page=13)).
- The wallet Supervisory Body is designated under Art. 46a(1) of Regulation (EU) 2014/910 and "shall be appointed by national law" ([§6.1, p. 14](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-03_Org_Tech_Proc_System_Owner_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_pub.pdf#page=14)).
- Under Art. 46a(5), if a wallet provider fails to comply with a remediation demand in time, the Supervisory Body may order suspension or complete cessation of wallet provision, communicated without undue delay to the Commission, other Member States' supervisory bodies, relying parties and end-users ([§6.1, p. 14](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-03_Org_Tech_Proc_System_Owner_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_pub.pdf#page=14)).
- The Supervisory Body submits an annual report on the preceding calendar year to the European Commission by March 31 (Art. 46a(6) and (7)) ([§6.1, p. 14](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-03_Org_Tech_Proc_System_Owner_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_pub.pdf#page=14)).
- The System Owner designates the Access Certificate Provider and the Provider of registration certificates "to designated QTSPs"; their trust anchors are published in a Trusted List by a designated Trusted List Provider and made available via the Commission's List of Trusted Lists ([§6.2, p. 14-15](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-03_Org_Tech_Proc_System_Owner_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_pub.pdf#page=14)).
- After successful certification, additional evaluation and verification by the Supervisory Authority, Wallet Providers are recognised in the NL Wallet eID Scheme and notified by NL to the EU to be put on a designated TrustList ([§6.3, p. 15](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-03_Org_Tech_Proc_System_Owner_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_pub.pdf#page=15)).
- On suspension or revocation the entity's Trusted List status becomes Invalid, and "all valid Wallet Unit Attestations (WUAs) associated with Wallet Units issued by that provider shall be revoked" ([§6.3, p. 15](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-03_Org_Tech_Proc_System_Owner_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_pub.pdf#page=15)).

Notifications to the European Commission
- CAB information (name and address; accreditation details and scope; modifications; withdrawal, suspension or termination of accreditation or authorisation) is communicated to the Commission by the NL Certification System Owner (Art. 20(1b) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1183) ([§7.1, p. 16](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-03_Org_Tech_Proc_System_Owner_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_pub.pdf#page=16)).
- Information on certified and admitted Wallet Providers is communicated by the Supervisory Authority; under Art. 3 of CIR (EU) 2024/2980 this includes the bodies providing wallets, the bodies associating PID with wallets, and the validation mechanisms for PID, relying parties and wallets ([§7.2, p. 16-17](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-03_Org_Tech_Proc_System_Owner_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_pub.pdf#page=16)).
- The Supervisory Authority notifies the Commission without undue delay of certification of a Wallet Solution (the top-level certificate) and its expiration, suspension or revocation, giving reasons for suspension or revocation; notifications are in English via the secure electronic notification system ([§7.3, p. 17](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-03_Org_Tech_Proc_System_Owner_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_pub.pdf#page=17)).

Complaints and information security
- Complaints are registered in the Complaint Management System hosted by the NL EUDIW Certification Scheme Owner ([§8, p. 18](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-03_Org_Tech_Proc_System_Owner_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_pub.pdf#page=18)).
- The System Owner adopts the cryptographic mechanisms specified in the relevant technical specifications in compliance with Annex I of CIR (EU) 2024/2979, and maintains and disseminates an inventory of applicable mechanisms for CABs and Applicants ([§9, p. 19](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-03_Org_Tech_Proc_System_Owner_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_pub.pdf#page=19)).

## Process

Technical vulnerability management (Figure 2 "Technical Vulnerability Management", p.9; swimlanes System Owner, Wallet Provider, CAB-CB, ENISA, EC, Other Member States, Cooperation Group):
1. Wallet Provider detects the vulnerability and conducts a Vulnerability Impact Analysis (Wallet Provider) ([§4.1, p. 9](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-03_Org_Tech_Proc_System_Owner_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_pub.pdf#page=9)).
2. CAB-CB produces/forwards the Vulnerability Impact Analysis Report and informs the Scheme Owner with all elements needed to understand impact, required changes and broader implications (CAB-CB) ([§4.1, p. 10](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-03_Org_Tech_Proc_System_Owner_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_pub.pdf#page=10)).
3. System Owner decides whether top-level certificate suspension is required; on YES, information about the suspension goes to the EC and the Cooperation Group; on NO, the flow goes directly to “Final information on the vulnerability”; based on the CAB-CB suspension decision, the top-level Certificate Scheme Owner notifies the European Commission (System Owner / top-level Scheme Owner) ([§4.1, p. 10](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-03_Org_Tech_Proc_System_Owner_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_pub.pdf#page=10)).
4.
