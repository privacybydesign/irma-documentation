## What this document is

NL-FS-W specifies the functional certification scope for wallet solutions within the common EUDI Wallet Certification System and the corresponding requirements for functional conformance, and provides a corresponding Implementation Conformance Statement (ICS) ([§1, p. 5](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS-W_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_-_Wallet_Solution_Conformance_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=5)). It identifies the documents forming the legal basis for certification ([§1, p. 5](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS-W_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_-_Wallet_Solution_Conformance_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=5)). It is one of the two principal scope documents of the functional certification scheme (the other is [NL-FS-P](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-fs-p)) and operates within the framework, methodology and evaluation rules of [NL-FS](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-fs) ([§1, p. 5](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS-W_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_-_Wallet_Solution_Conformance_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=5)).

## Scope and object of certification

- The object of certification is "a wallet solution provided by a wallet provider" ([§5, p. 9](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS-W_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_-_Wallet_Solution_Conformance_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=9)).
- The certification scope is the conformity of the functional behaviour of the wallet solution observable at its interfaces towards: (a) PID or attestation issuance services, (b) Relying Parties, (c) Users, (d) auxiliary components such as Trusted Lists, List of Trusted Entities, and certificate revocation services ([§5, p. 9](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS-W_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_-_Wallet_Solution_Conformance_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=9)).
- These interfaces are in scope "insofar they are supported by the wallet solution that is being certified" ([§5, p. 9](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS-W_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_-_Wallet_Solution_Conformance_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=9)).
- Legal derivation: Regulation (EU) No 910/2014 as amended by Regulation (EU) 2024/1183, in particular Article 5a and Article 5c(1); under Article 5c(1) the scope comprises the requirements of Article 5a(4), (5) and (8) and, where applicable, the standards and technical specifications referred to in Article 5a(24) ([§2, p. 6](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS-W_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_-_Wallet_Solution_Conformance_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=6)).

## Who it applies to

- Conformity assessment bodies: the document is intended for them ([§1, p. 5](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS-W_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_-_Wallet_Solution_Conformance_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=5)). During certification the certification body shall request the documentation for any Recommended feature that is not implemented ([§6.1, p. 10](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS-W_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_-_Wallet_Solution_Conformance_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=10)).
- Wallet providers preparing a wallet solution for certification: the document is intended for them ([§1, p. 5](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS-W_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_-_Wallet_Solution_Conformance_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=5)). Wallet providers seeking functional certification "shall complete this ICS" ([§7.1, p. 14](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS-W_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_-_Wallet_Solution_Conformance_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=14)); the wallet provider completes the Annex A template and submits it as part of the application for certification ([Annex A, p. 15](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS-W_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_-_Wallet_Solution_Conformance_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=15)); it shall declare whether the wallet solution implements each listed feature ([Annex A.3, p. 15](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS-W_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_-_Wallet_Solution_Conformance_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=15)). For a Recommended feature not implemented, it shall understand and weigh the implications and document these considerations ([§6.1, p. 10](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS-W_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_-_Wallet_Solution_Conformance_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=10)).

## Key points

- Chapter 6 lists the requirements regarding features of wallet solutions mandated in the legal instruments; these "enable modularity of the certification" and allow a wallet provider to indicate which legally mandated features are implemented ([§6.1, p. 10](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS-W_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_-_Wallet_Solution_Conformance_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=10)).
- Requirements are categorised by the CIR in which the feature is (primarily) discussed; the rightmost column gives the conformance level ([§6.1, p. 10](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS-W_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_-_Wallet_Solution_Conformance_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=10)).
- Conformance levels Optional, Recommended, Required "shall be interpreted as specified in RFC 2119" ([§6.1, p. 10](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS-W_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_-_Wallet_Solution_Conformance_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=10)).
- Recommended: "there may exist valid reasons in particular circumstances for a wallet provider to not implement such a feature in their wallet solution, but the wallet provider shall understand and carefully weigh the full implications before doing so and shall document these considerations"; the certification body shall request this documentation for any recommended feature not implemented ([§6.1, p. 10](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS-W_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_-_Wallet_Solution_Conformance_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=10)).
- The document does not itself define Required and Optional beyond the reference to RFC 2119 ([§6.1, p. 10](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS-W_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_-_Wallet_Solution_Conformance_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=10)).
- "Where a wallet solution implements a feature, it shall comply with all related functional requirements in the legal basis and in the corresponding standards mentioned therein" ([§6.1, p. 10](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS-W_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_-_Wallet_Solution_Conformance_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=10)).

Additionally, indicated conformance levels are subject to change" ([§6.1, p. 10](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS-W_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_-_Wallet_Solution_Conformance_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=10)).
