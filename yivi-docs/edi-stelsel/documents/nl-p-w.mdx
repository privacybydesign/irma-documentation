## What this document is

NL-P-W specifies the Wallet Provider Certification Scheme, one of the Certification Schemes within the NL EUDI Wallet Certification System, and defines the Certification Scheme (in the sense of ISO/IEC 17067) that accredited Certification Bodies (CABs) shall apply when certifying Wallet Providers ([§1, p. 5](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=5)). It establishes the service requirements (by reference to the subsidiary documents), the evaluation methodology (by reference to normative Annex B and the subsidiary documents) and the conformity assessment procedures ([§2, p. 6](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=6)). It is primarily addressed to CABs; Wallet Providers should read it together with the applicable NL-P-* subsidiary documents, which specify the substantive requirements they shall meet ([§1, p. 5](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=5)).

## Scope and object of certification

- The scheme is for Wallet Providers that are to be certified and that seek to be admitted to the Dutch National eID Scheme (“Stelsel”) ([§2, p. 6](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=6)).
- The object of certification "encompasses services that consists of provision and operation of wallet solutions and of the electronic identification schemes under which they are provided" ([§2, p. 6](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=6)).
- "In the context of this certification scheme, the object of certification is the wallet provider, comprising the organizational arrangements and processes of the Wallet Provider that deliver the wallet related services to individuals (in conjunction with the PID Provider)" ([§2, p. 6](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=6)).
- §6.1 states: "The object of certification under this scheme is the Provider the electronic identification system under which of European Digital Identity Wallets are provided, as referred to in Article 5c(1) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1183"; the certification covers both the scheme-level governance arrangements and the operational services provided by EUDI Wallet Provider and PID Provider ([§6.1, p. 10](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=10)).
- Annex B describes the object of certification as "the electronic identification system, elated services and processes" ([Annex B, B.2, p. 26](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=26)) and as "The electronic identification system and related services defined by NL-P-W" ([Annex B, B.3.2 Table 1, p. 27](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=27)).
- The evaluation scope covers services related to provision and operation of the EUDI Wallet solution in conjunction with the national eID scheme; as a minimum it covers [NL-P-01](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-p-01), [NL-P-02](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-p-02), [NL-P-03](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-p-03), NL-P-W04, NL-P-W04b and the presence of valid [NL-FS](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-fs) and NL-CS certificates covering the wallet solution(s) and version(s) in scope ([§7.2, p. 13](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=13)).
- Outsourced or subcontracted services are in the evaluation scope to the extent they are material to fulfilling the certification requirements ([§7.2, p. 13](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=13)).
- Acceptable external assurance evidence may reduce the extent of the CAB's own evaluation activities, but the scope of the certification shall not be reduced on that basis ([§7.2, p. 13](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=13)).

## Who it applies to

- Scheme Owner: owns and supervises the scheme, authorises CABs, maintains the certificate register and monitors the scheme (normative basis ISO/IEC 17067) ([§6.2.1 Table 2, p. 10](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=10)). Attends the kick-off meeting ([§8.3.1, p. 17](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=17)); receives notification of issued certificates and of suspensions, terminations and withdrawals, and updates the Certificate Register ([§8.7, p. 19](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=19); [§8.10, p. 21](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=21)); decides on extension of a suspension ([§8.10, p. 21](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=21)); may impose contractual arrangements ([§9.2, p. 23](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=23)). The cover names the Scheme Owner as Kingdom of the Netherlands, Minister of Economic Affairs ([cover, p. 2](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=2)).
- CAB: plans, performs and documents the evaluation; issues the certification decision and certificate of conformity; manages surveillance and recertification (normative basis ISO/IEC 17065; [NL-SYS-05](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-sys-05)) ([§6.2.1 Table 2, p. 10](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=10)). Shall be accredited by the National Accreditation Body under ISO/IEC 17065 with a scope covering NL-P-W and the subsidiary documents ([§6.2.2, p. 11](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=11)) and shall implement impartiality mechanisms per ISO/IEC 17065 ([§6.3, p. 11](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=11)). "In the context of NL-P-W certification scheme only a certification body is considered as a CAB" ([§6.1, p. 10](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=10)). Grants, suspends and withdraws the right to use the mark of conformity ([§6.7, p. 12](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=12)).
- Applicant / Certificate Holder: submits the eID scheme for certification, provides evaluation evidence and complies with post-certification obligations, including surveillance cooperation and change notification (normative basis NL-P-W) ([§6.2.1 Table 2, p. 10](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=10)).
- EUDI Wallet Provider: shall notify the Scheme Owner and its NL-P-W CAB without undue delay when an [NL-FS](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-fs), NL-CS or NL-P-W certificate is suspended, withdrawn or expires ([§6.4, p. 11](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=11)); shall cease using the mark of conformity without undue delay when its certificate is suspended or withdrawn ([§6.7, p. 12](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=12)); shall document its rationale where it considers a CIR 2024/2690 "where appropriate/applicable/to the extent feasible" requirement not appropriate, applicable or feasible ([§7.5, p. 15](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=15)).
- PID Provider: named on the certificate of conformity ("name of PID Provider Provider") ([§8.7, p. 19](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=19)); its personnel may provide statements, explanations and demonstrations as evidence ([§7.4, p. 14](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=14)).
- National Accreditation Body (NAB): accredits CABs under ISO/IEC 17065 for the relevant scope (normative basis Regulation (EC) No 765/2008) ([§6.2.1 Table 2, p. 11](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=11)).
- Market Surveillance Authorities: oversight of certified services in the market; "This role will be performed by the [NL-TL](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-tl) Scheme Owner" ([§6.2.1 Table 2, p. 11](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=11)).
- Audit roles in Annex B: Audit Team (one or more auditors appointed by the CAB or its subcontractor, supported by technical experts if necessary), Audit Team Leader (manages the audit and reports findings to the CAB), Technical Expert (provides specialist knowledge, does not act independently as an auditor) ([Annex B, B.3.2 Table 1, p. 27](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=27)). Technical experts shall not act as sole evaluator of conformity or take the certification decision ([Annex B, B.10.2, p. 32](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=32)); the Audit Team shall not issue the certification decision ([Annex B, B.12.4, p. 34](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=34)).

## Key points

- Scheme type: Type 6 model of EN ISO/IEC 17067 (initial testing and evaluation, surveillance of the service delivery, periodic re-evaluation), in accordance with Article 4(3)(a) of CIR (EU) 2024/2981 ([§6.6, p. 12](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=12)).
- Applicable requirement documents (Table 1): [NL-P-01](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-p-01) Organisational and technical requirements for Wallet & PID providers; [NL-P-02](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-p-02) Essential Entity Cyber Resilience Requirements; [NL-P-03](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-p-03) Development & Vulnerability Mngt Requirements; NL-P-W04 Wallet Onboarding & Lifecycle Mngt Requirements; NL-P-W04b Fraud Mngt Requirements ([§6.1 Table 1, p. 10](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=10)). The CAB assesses conformity against these ([§6.1, p. 10](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=10)); they "constitute the normative substance of the eID Certification Scheme" ([§6.5, p. 12](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=12)).
- How each applies: [NL-P-01](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-p-01) gives management and operational requirements; [NL-P-02](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-p-02) gives cyber resilience requirements "in the spirit of NIS2"; [NL-P-03](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-p-03) gives development and vulnerability management practices "in the spirit of the CRA"; NL-P-W04 gives requirements for the wallet onboarding process and lifecycle events "to ensure Wallet stay under control of the correct citizen"; NL-P-W04b gives requirements that "should additionally be met" to ensure the service has resistance against fraud modus operandi ([§6.5, p. 12](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=12)).
- [NL-FS](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-fs) and NL-CS certificates are assessed and certified independently from NL-P-W, but the resulting certificates "constitute a necessary condition for certification under NL-P-W" ([§6.4, p. 11](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=11)).
- The CAB shall verify that the Wallet Provider holds valid [NL-FS](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-fs) and NL-CS certificates covering the wallet solution(s) and version(s) in scope; the scheme allows the Wallet Provider to submit declarations that appropriate [NL-FS](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-fs) and NL-CS evaluation and certification processes are in place, including a declaration of conformity regarding correct integration with the NL PID provider services; the CAB assesses scope and currency but need not re-evaluate requirements already covered ([§6.4, p. 11](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=11)).
- Before issuing a certificate, the CAB shall verify valid [NL-FS](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-fs) and NL-CS certificates; it "shall not issue a NL-P-W certificate of conformity where any required [NL-FS](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-fs) or NL-CS certificate is absent, expired, suspended or withdrawn" ([§8.7, p. 19](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=19)).
- Evaluation methods: document review, interview, observation, technical testing, sampling (sampling methodology documented in the evaluation plan) ([§7.3, p. 14](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=14)).
- Evidence types include server-side audit trails and logs from the live operational environment (systems serving real users, as distinct from test, pilot or reference environments) ([§7.4, p. 14](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=14)); evidence is retained per [NL-SYS-01](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-sys-01) section 5.10 ([§7.4, p. 15](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=15)).
- Proportionality: requirements derived from CIR 2015/1502 Section 2.4 are commensurate to risks at assurance level High; CIR 2024/2690 requirements are applied taking account of the Wallet Provider's risk exposure (Article 2(2)) ([§7.5, p. 15](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=15)). The CAB considers the risk register (minimum content per [NL-SYS-04](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-sys-04)), with particular attention to risks from integration of the Wallet(s) with the NL PID-provider ([§7.5, p. 15](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=15)).
- Accepted external assurance evidence: ISO/IEC 27001 certificates from a certification body accredited by a Dutch Accreditation Body under ISO/IEC 17021-1; ETSI EN 319 411-1/-2 certificates; certificates or reports under European or national cybersecurity certification schemes (such as EUCC, or EN 17640-based if mutual-recognition rules exist); other recognised reports meeting §7.7 ([§7.6, p. 15-16](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=15)).
- Evaluation criteria for assurance evidence (per Annex VI and Annex II of CIR 2024/2981): issuer competence and impartiality, scope adequacy, timeliness, rigor and depth, findings and exceptions ([§7.7, p. 16](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=16)). An ISO/IEC 27001 certificate does not on its own suffice; the CAB performs its own evaluation of the specific controls required by the subsidiary documents ([§7.7, p. 16](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=16)).
- Reliance (full, partial or none) is formally determined by the CAB per [NL-SYS-01](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-sys-01) §5.3 and NL-P-W §7.6-7.7, not by the Audit Team ([Annex B, B.9.2, p. 31](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=31)).
- Certification process phases: preparation and application, evaluation, and certification ([§8.1, p. 17](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=17)).
- Audit model: the audit forms part of the NL-P-W evaluation phase ([Annex B, B.11, p. 32](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=32)). Initial and re-assessment audits "should normally comprise Stage 1 and Stage 2"; the sequence may be adapted where justified and permitted by [NL-SYS-05](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-sys-05) and the audit plan ([Annex B, B.11.1, p. 32](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=32)). Stage 1 (documentation and readiness review) is intended to understand the object of certification, confirm audit readiness and plan Stage 2; its results shall be documented and used to finalise the Stage 2 plan; potential nonconformities identified in Stage 1 are treated as areas of concern until supported by sufficient evidence ([Annex B, B.11.2, p. 32](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=32)). Stage 2 (implementation and operational effectiveness) shall collect objective evidence that applicable requirements are implemented and operate effectively, and shall include relevant sites and operational environments unless sufficient equivalent evidence is obtained and the use of remote or external assurance evidence is justified ([Annex B, B.11.3, p. 32](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=32)). Surveillance and special audits can use a targeted, risk-based sequence rather than the full Stage 1 and Stage 2 model ([Annex B, B.11.4, p. 33](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=33)).
- Sampling shall be risk-based and documented (population, selection method, sample size, items, limitations) ([Annex B, B.8.2, p. 30](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=30)); a sample-based multi-site approach requires common control and a common management and review framework ([Annex B, B.8.3, p. 30](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=30)).
- Applicability per requirement: Applicable or Not applicable (with documented and accepted rationale); not-applicable requirements receive no conformity result ([Annex B, B.12.1, p. 33](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=33)).
- Requirement-level results: Conforming; Major nonconformity (substantial security impact and/or substantial impact on achieving the intended service or process outcome); Minor nonconformity (no such substantial impact); Not concluded - insufficient evidence (not treated as conformity, must be resolved before a positive audit recommendation) ([Annex B, B.12.2 Table 3, p. 33](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=33)). Positive or negative observations may be recorded; an observation may not replace a conformity assessment result and shall not, by itself, constitute a failure to meet a requirement ([Annex B, B.12.3, p. 33-34](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=33)).

## Process

1. Applicant: submits, before evaluation starts, a description of the Wallet Provider's environment and systems; documentation of compliance with the subsidiary documents; valid [NL-FS](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-fs) and NL-CS certificates for the wallet solution version(s) in scope or declarations that [NL-FS](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-fs)/NL-CS processes are in place; any external assurance evidence; the completed CAB application form. The application may be separate or combined with other schemes per [NL-SYS-01](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-sys-01) ([§8.2, p. 17](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=17)).
2. CAB, Applicant and Scheme Owner representatives: kick-off meeting no later than 10 working days after receipt of a complete application; the CAB agrees the evaluation plan (scope, schedule, reliance approach, on-site visits and interviews) with the Applicant ([§8.3.1, p. 17](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=17)).
3. CAB: performs the evaluation per §7 and "the applicable WM-03 audit framework" and documents it in an Evaluation Report with an overall assessment of conformity with each requirement, the reliance determination for external assurance evidence and a recommendation for the certification decision ([§8.3.2, p. 18](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=18)); under Annex B, initial audits should normally comprise Stage 1 and Stage 2 ([Annex B, B.11.1, p. 32](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=32)). The CAB notifies the Applicant of substantial findings without undue delay; the Applicant may respond within the timeframe agreed at kick-off ([§8.3.2, p. 18](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=18)).
4. Applicant / CAB: correction of nonconformities; major nonconformities shall be corrected and their closure verified before a positive certification recommendation or decision ([Annex B, B.14, p. 34](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=34)).
5. CAB: after reviewing the evaluation results, certification decision and informing the Applicant within 5 working days; certificate issued no later than 14 working days after a positive decision ([§8.4, p. 18](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=18)).
6. Applicant / CAB: on a negative decision, written complaint within 14 working days; CAB final decision within 14 working days of receipt ([§8.4, p. 18](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=18)).
7. CAB: notifies the Scheme Owner of the issued certificate within five working days ([§8.7, p. 19](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=19)).
8. CAB: annual surveillance during validity ([§8.8, p. 19-20](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=19)).
9. Applicant / CAB: recertification, notified at least six months before expiry ([§8.9, p. 20-21](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=20)).
10. CAB / Scheme Owner: suspension, withdrawal or termination; CAB notifies Scheme Owner, who updates the Certificate Register ([§8.10, p. 21](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-W_Wallet_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=21)).
