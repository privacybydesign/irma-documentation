NL-SYS-04 is part of the system framework. It is listed in the publication of 6 November 2026 as *under construction*, and no PDF of it is published.

What the other documents say about it:

- <DocLink id="NL-SYS-01" /> Figure 3 lists it in the system framework as "Developing and maintaining the risk register for EUDI Wallet implementation and certification" <Cite doc="NL-SYS-01" page={15}>§4.2</Cite>.
- According to NL-SYS-01, the methodology for developing and maintaining the risk register is included in NL-SYS-04. Specific threats and risks are also considered in the security problem definitions of the protection profiles (NL-CS series) and in the audit framework documents (NL-P series) <Cite doc="NL-SYS-01" page={29}>§7.1.1</Cite>.
- <DocLink id="NL-TL" /> lists NL-SYS-04 as one of its parent documents <Cite doc="NL-TL" page={9}>§4.2</Cite>.

The risk register itself is defined in the [glossary](/edi-stelsel-certification/glossary#risk-register) as a "record of information relevant to the certification process about identified risks", with Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/2981, article 2(7) as its source.
