## What this document is

NL-P-P specifies the PID Provider Certification Scheme, one of the Certification Schemes within the NL EUDI Wallet Certification System, and defines the Certification Scheme (in the sense of ISO/IEC 17067) that the accredited and authorized Certification Body (CAB) shall apply when certifying the NL PID Provider ([§1, p. 6](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=6)). It establishes the evaluation methodology (by reference to normative Annex B), the service requirements (in the subsidiary documents) and the conformity assessment procedures ([§2, p. 7](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=7)). It is primarily addressed to the CAB; the PID Provider should read it with the applicable NL-P-* subsidiary documents, which specify the requirements it shall meet ([§1, p. 6](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=6)).

## Scope and object of certification

- The scheme is for the PID Provider that is to be certified as the "foundational trust anchor" of the Dutch National eID Scheme ("Stelsel") ([§2, p. 7](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=7)).
- "The object of certification is the PID Provider, comprising the organizational arrangements and processes of the PID Provider that are related to the issuance and lifecycle management of the PID to certified wallets of respective citizens" ([§2, p. 7](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=7)).
- The object of certification encompasses all services supporting controlled issuance and lifecycle management of PIDs and "transversal services that support the trust in the overall eID national scheme (like user dashboard and notification in order to combat fraud)" ([§2, p. 7](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=7)).
- §6.1: the object of certification is "the PID Provider that issue PIDs in the NL National eID scheme (“stelsel”), as referred to in Article 5c(1) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1183"; the certification covers both the scheme-level governance arrangements and the operational services provided by the PID Provider ([§6.1, p. 11](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=11)).
- Annex B describes the object of certification as "the electronic identification system, elated services and processes" ([Annex B, B.2, p. 27](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=27)) and "The electronic identification system and related services defined by NL-P-P" ([Annex B, B.3.2 Table 1, p. 28](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=28)).
- Evaluation scope: services related to provision and operation of PID Issuance and Lifecycle Management in the NL national eID scheme; as a minimum [NL-P-01](/edi-stelsel/documents/nl-p-01), [NL-P-02](/edi-stelsel/documents/nl-p-02), [NL-P-03](/edi-stelsel/documents/nl-p-03), NL-P-P04 and NL-P-P04b (the latter including transversal fraud-combating services such as user dashboard and notification) ([§7.2, p. 14](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=14)).
- Material outsourced or subcontracted services are in scope ([§7.2, p. 14](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=14)); external assurance evidence may reduce the CAB's own evaluation activity but not the certification scope ([§7.2, p. 14](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=14)).

## Who it applies to

- Scheme Owner: owns and supervises the scheme, authorises CABs, maintains the certificate register and monitors the scheme (ISO/IEC 17067) ([§6.2.1 Table 2, p. 11](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=11)). Attends the kick-off meeting ([§8.3.1, p. 18](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=18)); is notified of issued certificates and of suspension, termination or withdrawal and updates the Certificate Register ([§8.7, p. 20](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=20); [§8.10, p. 22](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=22)); decides on suspension extensions ([§8.10, p. 22](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=22)); may impose contractual arrangements ([§8.13, p. 22](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=22); [§9.2, p. 24](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=24)).
- CAB: plans, performs and documents the evaluation; issues the certification decision and certificate; manages surveillance and recertification (ISO/IEC 17065; [NL-SYS-05](/edi-stelsel/documents/nl-sys-05)) ([§6.2.1 Table 2, p. 11](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=11)). Accredited by the National Accreditation Body under ISO/IEC 17065 with a scope covering NL-P-P and subsidiary documents ([§6.2.2, p. 12](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=12)). "In the context of NL-P-P certification scheme only a certification body that is accredited and also authorized under Dutch law is eligible as CAB" ([§6.1, p. 11](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=11)). Implements impartiality mechanisms per ISO/IEC 17065 ([§6.3, p. 12](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=12)).
- Applicant / Certificate Holder: submits the eID scheme for certification, provides evidence, complies with post-certification obligations including surveillance cooperation and change notification (normative basis NL-P-P) ([§6.2.1 Table 2, p. 11](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=11)).
- PID Provider: shall document its rationale in a comprehensive manner where it considers a CIR 2024/2690 "where appropriate/applicable/to the extent feasible" requirement not appropriate, applicable or feasible ([§7.5, p. 16](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=16)); may request termination of the certificate ([§8.10, p. 22](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=22)).
- National Accreditation Body (NAB): accredits CABs under ISO/IEC 17065 (Regulation (EC) No 765/2008) ([§6.2.1 Table 2, p. 11](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=11)).
- Market Surveillance Authorities: oversight of certified services; "This role will be performed by the [NL-TL](/edi-stelsel/documents/nl-tl) Scheme Owner" ([§6.2.1 Table 2, p. 11](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=11)).
- Wallet Providers: certified under [NL-P-W](/edi-stelsel/documents/nl-p-w), co-exist in the same National eID Scheme and rely on the PID provider to provide a PID to their certified and admitted wallets ([§6.4, p. 12](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=12)). A suspension or revocation of the NL-P-P certificate has an immediate effect on their top-level certificates ([§6.7, p. 13](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=13); [§8.11, p. 22](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=22)).
- Audit roles (Annex B): Audit Team, Audit Team Leader, Technical Expert, with "Provider" defined as "PID Provider" ([Annex B, B.3.2 Table 1, p. 28](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=28)).

## Key points

- Scheme type: Type 6 model of EN ISO/IEC 17067, in accordance with Article 4(3)(a) of CIR (EU) 2024/2981 ([§6.6, p. 13](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=13)).
- Applicable requirement documents (Table 1): [NL-P-01](/edi-stelsel/documents/nl-p-01) Organisational and technical requirements for Wallet & PID providers; [NL-P-02](/edi-stelsel/documents/nl-p-02) Essential Entity Cyber Resilience Requirements; [NL-P-03](/edi-stelsel/documents/nl-p-03) Development & Vulnerability Mngt Requirements; NL-P-P04 PID Issuance & Lifecycle Mngt Requirements; NL-P-P04b Fraud Mngt Requirements ([§6.1 Table 1, p. 11](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=11)). They "constitute the normative substance of the eID Certification Scheme" ([§6.5, p. 12](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=12)).
- How each applies: [NL-P-01](/edi-stelsel/documents/nl-p-01) management and operational requirements; [NL-P-02](/edi-stelsel/documents/nl-p-02) cyber resilience "in the spirit of NIS2"; [NL-P-03](/edi-stelsel/documents/nl-p-03) development and vulnerability management "in the spirit of the CRA"; NL-P-P04 requirements during PID issuance and lifecycle events "to ensure PIDs are issued and remain under control of the correct citizen"; NL-P-P04b fraud resistance of the service and the overall NL national eID scheme ([§6.5, p. 12](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=12)).
- Marks: "The PID provider will not carry any mark of conformity. But any suspension or revocation will have an immediate effect on the top-level certificates of all Wallet Providers in the NL national eID scheme" ([§6.7, p. 13](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=13)).
- Evaluation methods: document review, interview, observation, technical testing, sampling ([§7.3, p. 15](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=15)).
- Evidence includes server-side audit trails and logs from the live operational environment (back-end systems for PID and attestation issuance and revocation, event logging, interfaces) and "valid certificates for the (Qualified) eSealing services it uses and/or QeSCDs it operates" ([§7.4, p. 15](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=15)).
- Proportionality: CIR 2015/1502 Section 2.4 requirements are commensurate to risks at assurance level High; CIR 2024/2690 requirements are applied according to the PID Provider's risk exposure (Article 2(2)) ([§7.5, p. 16](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=16)). The CAB considers the risk register ([NL-SYS-04](/edi-stelsel/documents/nl-sys-04)) when determining depth and focus ([§7.5, p. 16](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=16)).
- Accepted external assurance evidence: ISO/IEC 27001 certificates (ISO/IEC 17021-1 accredited body, Dutch Accreditation Body); ETSI EN 319 411-1/-2 certificates; certificates or reports under European or national cybersecurity schemes (such as EUCC); other recognised reports meeting §7.7 ([§7.6, p. 16](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=16)). Criteria: issuer competence and impartiality, scope adequacy, timeliness, rigor and depth, findings and exceptions; ISO/IEC 27001 alone is not sufficient for the specific controls ([§7.7, p. 16-17](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=16)).
- Audit model: initial and re-assessment audits "should normally comprise Stage 1 and Stage 2" ([Annex B, B.11.1, p. 33](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=33)); Stage 1 documentation and readiness review; Stage 2 implementation and operational effectiveness ([Annex B, B.11.2-B.11.3, p. 33-34](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=33)); the sequence may be adapted where justified and permitted by [NL-SYS-05](/edi-stelsel/documents/nl-sys-05) and the audit plan ([Annex B, B.11.1, p. 33](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=33)); Stage 1 results shall be documented and used to finalise the Stage 2 plan, and potential nonconformities identified during Stage 1 shall be treated as areas of concern until supported by sufficient evidence ([Annex B, B.11.2, p. 33](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=33)); surveillance and special audits "can use a targeted, risk-based sequence rather than the full Stage 1 and Stage 2 model" ([Annex B, B.11.4, p. 34](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=34)).
- Sampling shall be risk-based and documented ([Annex B, B.8.2, p. 31](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=31)); multi-site sampling only under common control and management framework ([Annex B, B.8.3, p. 31](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=31)).
- Applicability: each requirement in the assessment matrix is determined Applicable or Not applicable (rationale for exclusion documented and accepted); a not-applicable requirement is not assigned a conformity assessment result ([Annex B, B.12.1, p. 34](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=34)).
- Requirement-level results ([Annex B, B.12.2 Table 3, p. 34](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=34)):
  - Conforming: sufficient and appropriate objective evidence demonstrates fulfilment of the specified requirement.
  - Major nonconformity: a non-fulfilment of a specified requirement that has a substantial security impact and/or a substantial impact on the ability of the object of certification to achieve the intended service or process outcome.
  - Minor nonconformity: a non-fulfilment that does not have any substantial security impact and does not have any substantial impact on the ability of the object of certification to achieve the intended service or process outcome.
  - Not concluded - insufficient evidence: sufficient and appropriate evidence was not available to determine conformity; "shall not be treated as conformity and shall be resolved before a positive audit recommendation is issued in relation to the applicable scope".
- Observations (positive or negative) may be recorded in addition; an observation "may not replace a conformity assessment result and shall not, by itself, constitute a failure to meet a requirement" ([Annex B, B.12.3, p. 35](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=35)). The Audit Team shall not issue the certification decision; it is made by the CAB ([Annex B, B.12.4, p. 35](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=35)).
- Major nonconformities shall be corrected and closure verified before a positive certification recommendation or decision; minor nonconformity timeframes are agreed per NL-P-P, "G-SYS-05" and CAB procedure; the framework "does not establish an independent fixed deadline" ([Annex B, B.14, p. 35](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=35)). For each nonconformity the Applicant or Certificate Holder shall provide correction, root-cause analysis, corrective action, responsible owner, target date and evidence of completion, as appropriate to its significance; the CAB evaluates the adequacy of proposed corrective actions and determines the verification activities required for closure ([Annex B, B.14, p. 35](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=35)).
- Special audits shall be initiated by the CAB in case of significant changes, security incidents, complaints, failure to implement corrective actions or other information relevant to continued conformity ([Annex B, B.15, p. 36](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=36)).
- Certification process phases: preparation and application phase, evaluation phase, certification phase ([§8.1, p. 18](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=18)).
- Time limits: kick-off no later than 10 working days after a complete application ([§8.3.1, p. 18](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=18)); certification decision and informing the Applicant within 5 working days; certificate issued no later than 14 working days after a positive decision ([§8.4, p. 19](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=19)); complaint against a negative decision within 14 working days, final CAB decision within 14 working days of receipt ([§8.4, p. 19](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=19)); Scheme Owner notified of issued certificate within five working days ([§8.7, p. 20](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=20)).
- Planned CAB effort (initial certification; maximum and planned, changeable by agreement with the Scheme Owner): Evaluation 30 working days; Certification 15 working days ([§8.5 Table 3, p. 19](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=19)).
- Certificate valid for four years from issuance ([§8.6, p. 19](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=19)); certificate states validity "not to exceed four years" ([§8.7, p. 20](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=20)).
- Certificate content: object of certification; scope with explicit reference to each NL-P-P subsidiary document; name of the PID Provider; "the version(s) of the wallet solutions in scope"; validity period; conditions, limitations or exceptions ([§8.7, p. 20](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=20)).
- Surveillance: annual throughout the validity of the certificate, including at a minimum review of material changes to the PID issuance & lifecycle management services and/or NL national eID scheme fraud detection measures, its components or organisational arrangements as notified to the CAB; review of incidents or security breaches as notified to the CAB; review of updates to NL-P-P and subsidiary documents and their implications; targeted evaluation activities determined by the CAB based on the risk register and intelligence gathered; follow-up on non-conformities, observations or conditions previously identified ([§8.8, p. 20](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=20)).

## Process

1. Applicant: submits, before evaluation, a description of the PID Provider's environment and systems; documentation of compliance with NL-P-P subsidiary documents; valid certificates for any (Qualified) eSealing services it has and/or QeSCDs it operates to e-seal PIDs; any external assurance evidence; the completed CAB application form. The application may be separate or combined with other schemes per [NL-SYS-01](/edi-stelsel/documents/nl-sys-01) ([§8.2, p. 18](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=18)).
2. CAB, Applicant and Scheme Owner representatives: kick-off meeting no later than 10 working days after a complete application; CAB agrees the evaluation plan with the Applicant ([§8.3.1, p. 18](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=18)).
3. CAB (Audit Team): evaluation per §7 and Annex B (Stage 1, Stage 2), documented in an Evaluation Report ([§8.3.2, p. 18-19](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=18); [Annex B, B.11, p. 33](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=33)). CAB notifies substantial findings without undue delay; Applicant may respond within the agreed timeframe ([§8.3.2, p. 19](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=19)).
4. Applicant / CAB: nonconformity correction; major nonconformities closed before a positive decision ([Annex B, B.14, p. 35](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=35)).
5. CAB: decision and informing the Applicant within 5 working days; certificate no later than 14 working days after a positive decision ([§8.4, p. 19](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=19)).
6. Applicant / CAB: complaint within 14 working days of a negative decision; CAB final decision within 14 working days ([§8.4, p. 19](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=19)).
7. CAB: notifies the Scheme Owner within five working days of issuance ([§8.7, p. 20](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=20)).
8. CAB: annual surveillance ([§8.8, p. 20-21](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=20)).
9. Applicant / CAB: recertification, notified at least six months before expiry ([§8.9, p. 21](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=21)).
10. CAB / Scheme Owner: suspension, withdrawal or termination; Scheme Owner updates the Certificate Register ([§8.10, p. 22](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-P_PID_Provider_Certification_Scheme_RC1.1-EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publ.pdf#page=22)).
