## What this document is

NL-SYS-01 is "a high-level framework that explains the EUDI Wallet certification system, ensuring its conformity with related regulations" ([§1, p. 5](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=5)). The certification system is based on the concept and content of the certification system described in ISO/IEC 17067 ([§1, p. 5](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=5)). It aims to provide interested parties with information on top-level certificate issuance for European Digital Identity Wallets and the NL electronic identification scheme under which they are provided, in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2014/910 modified by 2024/1183 Article 5d(1) ([§1, p. 5](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=5)). It gives an overview of how the structure of schemes and its elements are nested and meet the requirements of the relevant standards and regulations, to reach a top-level European Digital Identity Wallet certification ([§4.2, p. 15](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=15)).

## Scope and object of certification

- The NL EUDI Wallet Certification System addresses the object of certification defined in CIR 2024/2981, art. 3(2) and 3(3) ([§4.2, p. 14](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=14)).
- It consists of the general approach of assessing the conformity of European Digital Identity Wallets and the electronic identification scheme under which they are provided as defined in CIR 2024/2981 ([§4.2, p. 14](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=14)).
- The legally required overall certification of "the digital identity wallet and the eID scheme under which the wallet is provided" is defined as one top-level scheme composing certificates from three distinct subordinate certification schemes: the European Digital Identity Wallet Functional Certification Scheme, the European Digital Identity Wallet Cybersecurity Certification Scheme (both labelled "NL-CS series" in the text), and the National eID Scheme Certification Scheme ([NL-P-P](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-p-p) and [NL-P-W](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-p-w) series) ([§4.2, p. 14-15](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=14)).
- The object of certification under CIR 2024/2981 art. 3(3) is mapped to the schemes as follows: software components by NL-FS-series, NL-CS-series (incl. the Wallet Instance FITCEM PP and WSCA PP) and resilience requirements in the [NL-P-P](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-p-p)/W-series; hardware components by "a Protection Profile for WSCD, certified externally"; processes that support provision and operation of a wallet solution referenced in CIR (EU) 2015/1502 by the [NL-P-P](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-p-p)/W-series, notably "Wallet Onboarding & LifeCycle Management" and "PID-issuance and lifecycle management" ([§7.1, p. 29](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=29)).
- Under "Certification System v1" only HSM-based architectures are accepted: the Wallet Instance runs on a mobile platform that meets established conditions and interacts with a Wallet Provider Backend where the HSM-based WSCA/WSCD run; and the Wallet Instance complies with the Wallet Instance FITCEM PP and the WSCA CC PP as defined under the NL-CS-series ([§Annex B, p. 39](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=39)).

## Who it applies to

- System Owner — "Top-level governance body of the certification system"; owner: Minister of Economic Affairs (in close concertation with Minister of the Interior) ([§3.1.1, p. 9](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=9)). Responsibilities: define the overall rules of the certification system; define the overall evaluation methods and requirements; maintain and update system documentation and make it available to all relevant stakeholders; ensure the overall system complies with ISO/IEC 17067 and legal requirements; notify the certification system to the EDICG for opinions and recommendations; cooperate with other EU Member States operating an EUDI Wallet certification system "to ensure a level playing field towards a future EU certification system" ([§3.1.1, p. 9](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=9)). Performs periodic reviews of the certification system and the schemes operating under it ([§5.11, p. 24](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=24)).
- Top-Level Scheme Owner — governance of the Top Level Certification Scheme; owner: Minister of Economic Affairs (in close concertation with Minister of the Interior) ([§3.1.2, p. 9](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=9)). Responsibilities: define elaborate scheme requirements; maintain scheme-specific documentation and make it available; ensure compliance with NL Certification System rules/methods/requirements; supervision of certification activities; compliance of certified services with national and EU legal frameworks; post-certification monitoring, particularly following incidents or vulnerabilities ([§3.1.2, p. 9](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=9)). The roles of System Owner and Top-Level Scheme Owner "can be combined" ([§3.1.2, p. 9](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=9)). Publishes a list of certified EUDI Wallet solutions with the information specified in CIR (EU) 2024/2981 Annex V and keeps it accurate and current ([§4.1, p. 14](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=14)).
- Subordinate Scheme Owners — governance of subordinate certification schemes for specific products or services ([§3.1.3, p. 10](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=10)). For some subordinate schemes individual CABs may propose their own scheme if legal requirements allow and the scheme fully complies with the NL Certification System, top-level scheme and set requirements; where EUCC is available and mandated, the legal ownership of the EUCC scheme applies ([§3.1.3, p. 10](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=10)). Responsibilities: define practical scheme details and operations; maintain scheme-specific documentation; ensure compliance with the NL Certification System and all overarching schemes; supervision of certification activities; post-certification monitoring ([§3.1.3, p. 10](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=10)).
- All Scheme Owners (Top-Level and subordinate) — see the obligations in §4.1 listed under Key rules ([§4.1, p. 13-14](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=13)).
- National Accreditation Body (NAB) — "Ensures trust and competence of Conformity Assessment Bodies (CABs)"; accredits CAB-CB according to ISO/IEC 17065 "or other standards, if required by the scheme"; accredits CAB-ITSEF according to ISO/IEC 17025 in respective certification schemes; verifies competence, impartiality and consistent application of certification rules; acts under Regulation 765/2008 ([§3.1.4, p. 10](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=10)).
- Conformity Assessment Body (CAB-CB) — "Performs or subcontracts conformity assessment activities"; conducts or subcontracts evaluations, audits or testing; produces or validates test reports, evaluation reports and certification documentation; supports decision-making on conformity findings ([§3.1.5, p. 10](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=10)). Footnote: in EUCC two types of CABs are recognised, CAB-CB (certification bodies) and CAB-ITSEF (testing laboratories) ([§4.3, p. 17](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=17)).
- Conformity Assessment Body (CAB-ITSEF) — "Performs evaluation or testing activities"; conducts evaluations or testing; produces test reports, evaluation reports and evaluation documentation; supports the CAB-CB decision-making ([§3.1.6, p. 11](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=11)).
- Applicant / Certificate Holder — an entity seeking certification or possessing the certificate, "Typically, the EUDI Wallet provider, PID provider or their subcontractors and suppliers"; submits the application; provides all required technical, procedural and security documentation; continuously maintains conformity including post-certification obligations ([§3.1.7, p. 11](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=11)). Each applicant/holder is responsible for the costs of the certification process and maintenance of its own certificate ([§3.1.7, p. 11](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=11)).
- Wallet Provider — must, for the top-level certificate, satisfy the applicable requirements of the scheme, provide the required underlying certificates and demonstrate that it meets the national admission-conditions set in National law ([§1, p. 5](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=5)); when applying for the top-level certificate it submits all relevant certificates and certification reports of the subordinate schemes ([§5.2, p. 19](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=19)).
- PID Provider — must hold an active certificate for the top-level certificate to be issued ([§1, p. 7](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=7)).
- European Digital Identity Cooperation Group (EDICG) — EU-level coordination and harmonisation; supports consistent implementation across Member States; facilitates cooperation between national authorities incl. supervision, incident handling and cross-border interoperability; provides guidance for uniform application of certification-related requirements; assists the European Commission in monitoring and improving the functioning of the European Digital Identity ecosystem ([§3.1.8, p. 11](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=11)).
- Supervisory Authority (SA) — "Supervisory and oversight function, according to EU and national law"; responsibilities: supervision of Trust Framework (see [NL-SYS-03](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-sys-03)); cross-border cooperation and notification processes ([§3.1.9, p. 11-12](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=11)). "SA may not be merged with the System/Scheme Owner role. Different organizational units of the Ministry of Digital Affairs have been assigned to these roles." ([§3.1.9, p. 11](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=11)).
- Users of an EUDI Wallet — may appeal certification decisions ([§5.9, p. 23](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=23)).

## Key points

Certification system architecture and document hierarchy
- The system is a layered, modular architecture in which functional, cybersecurity and eID certification schemes "combine to form the overall EUDI Wallet Provider Certificate (which will be issued per Wallet Provider)" ([§1, p. 6](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=6)).
- Functional certification of the Wallet Solution covers wallet functionality per Regulation (EU) 2014/910 modified by 2024/1183 and Implementing Acts, plus onboarding flow and PID issuance flow requirements; activities "mostly consist of product evaluation with functional testing" ([§1, p. 6](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=6)).
- Cybersecurity certification of the Wallet Solution consists of at least two external components: 1) the WSCA certificate and the WSCD certificate, both following EUCC, and 2) cybersecurity evaluation of the Wallet Unit (Wallet Instance with WSCA/WSCD) based on a FITCEM evaluation scheme (EN 17640:2022); activities "mostly consist of product evaluation with security testing" ([§1, p. 6](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=6)).
- Wallet provider certification and PID provider certification cover technical, procedural and organisational requirements for these providers outside the product certifications; activities "mostly consist of process evaluation and auditing" ([§1, p. 6](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=6)).
- The NL Top Level Certification is "the certificate The Netherlands needs to provide to Europe for each combination of wallet and eID-scheme (PID-provider)"; it "can only be issued if *and* the PID Provider has an activate certificate *and* the Wallet Provider has an active certificate *and* the Wallet provider demonstrates that it meets the national admission-conditions as set in National law" ([§1, p. 7](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=7)).
- All underlying certificates and underlying certification schemes "shall comply with ISO/IEC 17067 and ISO/IEC 17065" ([§1, p. 5](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=5)).
- Document hierarchy per Figure 3 ([§4.2, p. 15](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=15)):
  - System Framework: NL-SYS-01 Certification system; [NL-SYS-02](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-sys-02) Vocabulary; [NL-SYS-03](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-sys-03) Organization and support for the owner of the national EUDI certification scheme; [NL-SYS-04](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-sys-04) Developing and maintaining the risk register for EUDI Wallet implementation and certification; [NL-SYS-05](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-sys-05) Accreditation requirements for CABs.
  - TL Certification Scheme: [NL-TL](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-tl) – National Top-Level EUDIW Scheme.
  - Subordinate Schemes: EUDI Wallet Solution Functional Certification Scheme ([NL-FS](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-fs)); EUDI Wallet Solution Cybersecurity Certification Scheme (NL-CS); eID Scheme Certification Scheme Wallet Provider ([NL-P-W](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-p-w)); eID Scheme Certification Scheme PID Provider ([NL-P-P](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-p-p)).
  - Scheme requirements: under [NL-FS](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-fs): NL-FS-PP Issuance Requirements, NL-FS-Wallet Presentation Requirements; under NL-CS: [NL-CS-WSCA](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-cs-wsca) NL WSCA CC Protection Profile, [NL-CS-WI](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-cs-wi) NL Wallet Instance FITCEM Protect.Profile; shared by [NL-P-W](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-p-w) and [NL-P-P](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-p-p): [NL-P-01](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-p-01) Organisational and technical requirements for Wallet & PID providers, [NL-P-02](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-p-02) Essential Entity Requirements, [NL-P-03](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-p-03) Development & Vulnerability Mngt Requirements; NL-P-W04 Wallet Onboarding & Lifecycle Mngt Requirements ([NL-P-W](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-p-w)); NL-P-P04 PID Issuance & Lifecycle Mngt Requirements ([NL-P-P](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-p-p)).
- The structure is divided into (a) the Top-Level Scheme, (b) the sub-schemes for Wallet Solution functional certification, Wallet Solution cybersecurity certification and conformity with the National eID scheme rules, and (c) the subordinate specific requirements for each sub-scheme ([§4.2, p. 16](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=16)).
- ISO/IEC 17067 scheme types: Functional Certification Scheme Type 5; Cybersecurity Certification Scheme Type 5; National eID Scheme Certification Scheme Type 6 ([§4.2, p. 15](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=15)). NL-SYS-01 also states that "the certification scheme is a type 6 scheme for the top-level certificate ([NL-TL](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-tl))" ([§7.1, p. 30](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=30)).
- [NL-FS](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-fs) establishes "one certificate /declaration of conformity for a specific wallet solution as provided by a wallet provider" ([§4.2, p. 15](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=15)); the eID Certification Scheme ([NL-P-P](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-p-p)/[NL-P-W](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-p-w)) "will reuse some of the results obtained after performing the respective wallet solution certification (cybersecurity and functional certification)" ([§4.2, p. 16](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=16)).
- Terms "declaration of conformity, certificate, certificate of conformity" are synonyms in the context of the EUDI Wallet certification scheme; "EUDI wallet" and "European Digital Identity Wallet" are synonyms; "scheme" and "program" are synonyms (ISO/IEC 17000) ([§4.3, p. 17](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=17); [§1, p. 5](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=5)).

Accreditation (Table 1) ([§4.3, p. 16](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=16))
- Main evaluation activities: [NL-FS](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-fs) Testing; NL-CS Testing; [NL-P-P](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-p-p)/W Audit.
- Accreditation standard for CAB-CB: [NL-FS](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-fs) ISO/IEC 17065; NL-CS ISO/IEC 17065 augmented by EUCC program, where applicable; [NL-P-P](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-p-p)/W ISO/IEC 17065 augmented by ETSI EN 319 403-1 V2.3.1.
- Accreditation standard for CAB-ITSEF: [NL-FS](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-fs) ISO/IEC 17025; NL-CS ISO/IEC 17025 augmented by EUCC program, where applicable; [NL-P-P](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-p-p)/W "not relevant".
- Certification shall be performed in compliance with ISO/IEC 17065 (CIR 2024/2981 Art. 9(1)); evaluation techniques include audit, inspection, demonstration and test ([§4.3, p. 16](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=16)).
- Any CAB must pass an accreditation process within the scope of the relevant scheme and inform the Scheme Owner of its accreditation status and any change without undue delay; in duly justified cases a CAB may start evaluation or certification services according to [NL-SYS-05](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-sys-05), Section 10 ([§5.1, p. 19](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=19)).

Scheme Owner obligations
- Under CIR 2024/2981 Art. 3(1) Member States assign a Scheme Owner for each national certification system; the owner of the top-level scheme is the Minister of Economic Affairs (in close concertation with the Minister of the Interior) ([§4.1, p. 13](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=13)).
- The Top-Level Scheme Owner and subordinate Scheme Owners “need to”, among other things: take on full responsibility for the objectives, content and integrity of the scheme; maintain the scheme and provide guidance when required; set up a structure for its operation and management; document its content; ensure it is developed by people competent in technical and conformity assessment aspects; plan to protect the confidentiality of information provided by the parties involved; "operate and maintain the risk register defined in CIR (EU) 2024/2981"; manage risks/liabilities; approve the scope of subcontracting by the certification body ([§4.1, p. 13](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=13)).
- Scheme Owners may subcontract a defined part of their tasks to a third party; they shall set out the duties and responsibilities of all parties by contract and shall remain responsible for all subcontracted activities ([§4.1, p. 13](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=13)).
- Scheme Owners shall monitor the compliance of CAB-CBs with their obligations and shall perform this monitoring, if applicable, at least based on information from CAB-CBs, CAB-ITSEFs, national accreditation bodies and relevant market surveillance authorities (if applicable), their own or another authority’s audits and investigations, and complaints and appeals received ([§4.1, p. 13](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=13)).
- Scheme Owners inform the NL Certification System / NL-Top-Level Scheme Owner of scheme revisions; where applicable, the latter notifies changes to the EDICG ([§4.1, p. 14](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=14)).

Certification process rules
- Top-Level Certification of the EUDI Wallet Provider is performed under [NL-TL](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-tl), building on certificates or declarations of conformity from [NL-TL](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-tl), [NL-FS](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-fs), NL-CS, [NL-P-P](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-p-p)/W ([§5.2, p. 19](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=19)).
- The CAB-CB shall assess scope and validity of the submitted subordinate certificates and verify that the applicant meets the conditions to be recognised/admitted as a wallet provider under the NL Wallet eID-scheme before issuing a top-level certificate ([§5.2, p. 19](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=19)).
- External assurance evidence: the CAB-CB records Full, Partial or No reliance per piece of evidence; with partial reliance it performs supplementary evaluation; with no reliance it evaluates the requirement in full; the CAB-CB "remains fully responsible for the certification decision" ([§5.3, p. 19-20](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=19)). Acceptance may not contradict ISO/IEC 17065, 7.4.5 ([§5.3, p. 20](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=20)).
- The top-level certificate is subject to annual surveillance by the CAB-CB ([§5.4, p. 20](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=20)).
- Validity period of the top-level certificate and of wallet provider and PID-provider certificates: four years (see [NL-TL](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-tl), [NL-P-P](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-p-p)/W) ([§5.10, p. 23](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=23)).
- The retention period of certification records “shall be defined according to relevant legal requirements”, which will include provisions so that records related to [NL-TL](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-tl), [NL-P-P](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-p-p)/W and [NL-FS](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-fs)/CS certification are, in addition to local backups, also given in custody at the Certification System Owner; NOTE 1 recommends a retention period of not less than five years after the certification validity has expired ([§5.10, p. 24](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=24)).

Certificate holder obligations ([§5.5, p. 20-21](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=20))
- Comply with European and national legislation incl. CIR 2024/2981; maintain communication channels with CAB-CBs, CAB-ITSEFs and the Scheme/System Owner; make essential information on secure use, certification and updates available to end users; run continual vulnerability identification and handling and cooperate with the CAB-CB and Scheme Owner; publish or reference publicly known vulnerabilities (identifying the vulnerability, affected versions and mitigations, without exploitable details); use the mark of conformity according to [NL-SYS-03](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-sys-03).

Changes, non-conformities, suspension, appeals
- Changes affecting the top-level certificate are handled under the CAB-CB's regular change management process; if initiated by the Certificate Holder, the CAB-CB obtains an Impact Analysis Report (IAR); changes resulting from a security incident or vulnerability are handled with respect to the assurance continuity principle ([§5.6, p. 21-22](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=21)).
- On holder non-compliance the CAB-CB formally notifies the holder, requests clarification and, if insufficient, sets a maximum period "which may not exceed thirty (30) calendar days" to propose corrective measures; on failure the CAB-CB enters the suspension procedure ([§5.7, p. 22](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=22)).
- Withdrawal or termination on request of the holder is final; withdrawal of the top-level certificate does not automatically invalidate component certificates that remain compliant; a suspended certificate may be reinstated through a formal certification process ([§5.8, p. 23](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=23)).
- Expiry of one Wallet Provider's or Wallet Solution's top-level certificate has no impact on other certificates under the system ([§5.8, p. 23](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=23)).
- Any certification decision may be appealed by a relevant stakeholder or by a user of an EUDI Wallet; appeals are handled by the CAB-CB under its public procedures; "The Scheme Owner may not alter CAB decisions"; any stakeholder may submit a complaint to the Scheme Owner about any aspect of the system ([§5.9, p. 23](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=23)).
- The certification schemes shall contain provisions that all complaints and appeals not resolved by the CAB-CB are sent to the Scheme Owner for assessment and resolution ([§4.1, p. 14](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=14)).

Marks

## Process

Top-level certification (as described in NL-SYS-01; details are delegated to [NL-TL](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-tl)):
1. CAB obtains accreditation in the scope of the relevant scheme and informs the Scheme Owner (CAB) ([§5.1, p. 19](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=19)).
2. Wallet Provider submits the application for the top-level certificate with all relevant subordinate certificates and certification reports (Wallet Provider) ([§5.2, p. 19](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=19)).
3. CAB-CB assesses scope and validity of those certificates, determines reliance on external assurance evidence (full/partial/none) and verifies the admission conditions under the NL Wallet eID-scheme before issuing a top-level certificate (CAB-CB) ([§5.2–5.3, p. 19-20](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=19)).
4. Top-Level Scheme Owner publishes the list of certified EUDI Wallet solutions (CIR 2024/2981 Annex V) (Top-Level Scheme Owner) ([§4.1, p. 14](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=14)).
5. Annual surveillance of the top-level certificate (CAB-CB) ([§5.4, p. 20](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=20)).
6. Changes: holder notifies CAB-CB with an IAR; CAB-CB decides on re-evaluation; maintenance report sent to the System/Scheme Owner (Certificate Holder, CAB-CB) ([§5.6, p. 21](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=21)).
7. Non-compliance: CAB-CB notification, clarification, corrective-measure period of at most 30 calendar days, then suspension procedure (CAB-CB) ([§5.7, p. 22](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=22)).
8. Expiry: CAB informs the Certificate Holder, System Owner and top-level scheme owner of the expiration date and takes action toward re-certification (CAB) ([§5.8, p. 23](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-01_EUDI_Wallet_System_RC1.1_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=23)).
