## What this document is

NL-P-04W addresses the lifecycle events of Wallet Instances and Wallet Units and establishes the security requirements that Wallet Providers must implement so that wallet onboarding and lifecycle management processes meet the required security level ([§1.1, p. 6](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04W_Wallet_Onboarding__Lifecycle_Mngt_-_reqs_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=6)). It is intended as a normative basis for conformity assessment of EUDIW solutions with respect to wallet onboarding and lifecycle management, to be used by Wallet Providers for implementation and by CABs for conformity assessment ([§1.1, p. 6](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04W_Wallet_Onboarding__Lifecycle_Mngt_-_reqs_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=6)). The requirements are risk-based, derived from legislation, reference frameworks and threat models; a companion risk register is maintained as a separate deliverable ([§1.1, p. 6](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04W_Wallet_Onboarding__Lifecycle_Mngt_-_reqs_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=6)).

## Scope and object of certification

- Defines the normative requirements for Wallet Providers for secure onboarding and lifecycle management of Wallet Instances and Wallet Units; it is "a subsidiary requirements specification under [NL-P-W](/edi-stelsel/documents/nl-p-w)" ([§1.2, p. 6](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04W_Wallet_Onboarding__Lifecycle_Mngt_-_reqs_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=6)).
- "The object addressed by this specification is the Wallet Provider's onboarding and lifecycle-management service and its controlled interaction with the Wallet Instance, Wallet Unit, WSCA/WSCD, Wallet User, PID Provider and other relevant ecosystem parties" ([§1.2, p. 7](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04W_Wallet_Onboarding__Lifecycle_Mngt_-_reqs_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=7)).
- Lifecycle events in scope: Wallet Instance Installation; Wallet Unit Activation; Operational State (Without PID); PID Storage and Transition to Valid State; Wallet Unit Management and Maintenance; Device Change / Migration; Uninstallation; Revocation (Permanent) ([§1.2, p. 7](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04W_Wallet_Onboarding__Lifecycle_Mngt_-_reqs_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=7)).
- Out of scope: Wallet Solutions where more than one Wallet Instance per Wallet Unit can be active at the same time; PID Provider processes (identity verification and PID issuance); lifecycle events of the Wallet Solution (build, versioning, solution suspension and revocation); Wallet Solution-level organisational, policy, hardware, software and infrastructure requirements; EAA lifecycle events; Relying Party requirements; cross-Member-State interoperability ([§1.2, p. 7](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04W_Wallet_Onboarding__Lifecycle_Mngt_-_reqs_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=7)).
- A Wallet Instance is the software application installed on a Wallet User's device; a Wallet Unit is the personalised, Wallet User-bound instance of that software which has undergone activation and binding to the Wallet User's cryptographic material and identity ([§1.1, p. 6](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04W_Wallet_Onboarding__Lifecycle_Mngt_-_reqs_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=6)). §3.1 defines a Wallet Unit as "A unique configuration of a Wallet Solution that includes one or more Wallet Instances, Wallet Secure Cryptographic Applications and Wallet Secure Cryptographic Devices, provided by a Wallet Provider to an individual Wallet User" ([§3.1, p. 10](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04W_Wallet_Onboarding__Lifecycle_Mngt_-_reqs_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=10)).

## Who it applies to

- Wallet Provider: must implement the requirements ([§1.1, p. 6](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04W_Wallet_Onboarding__Lifecycle_Mngt_-_reqs_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=6)); 170 of the 201 requirements begin "The Wallet Provider shall" (counted), e.g. [INST-5.1-01](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-04w#inst-5-1-01) ([§5.1, p. 17](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04W_Wallet_Onboarding__Lifecycle_Mngt_-_reqs_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=17)).
- Wallet Unit / Wallet Instance: some requirements are formulated for the Wallet Unit, e.g. "The Wallet Unit shall obtain the registration certificate of a PID Provider exclusively from the credential issuer metadata" ([PID-8.1-01](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-04w#pid-8-1-01)) ([§8.1, p. 51](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04W_Wallet_Onboarding__Lifecycle_Mngt_-_reqs_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=51)); the specification defines controls that "the Wallet Provider and Wallet Unit shall apply at their side" of interactions with the PID Provider ([§1.2, p. 7](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04W_Wallet_Onboarding__Lifecycle_Mngt_-_reqs_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=7)).
- PID Provider: "Requirements allocated to the PID Provider remain outside the Wallet Provider's responsibility" ([§1.2, p. 7](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04W_Wallet_Onboarding__Lifecycle_Mngt_-_reqs_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=7)). The Wallet Provider does not assume the PID Provider's responsibilities for identity proofing, identity verification or PID issuance ([§4.2, p. 15](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04W_Wallet_Onboarding__Lifecycle_Mngt_-_reqs_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=15)).
- CAB: the requirements "shall consequently be assessed both individually and in their lifecycle context"; the CAB shall evaluate whether the Wallet Provider's implementation maintains the required security properties across the applicable lifecycle transitions and whether alternative, exceptional and recovery paths introduce opportunities to bypass controls ([§4.1, p. 15](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04W_Wallet_Onboarding__Lifecycle_Mngt_-_reqs_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=15)). The CAB shall assess the requirements from the high-assurance perspective; the assessment shall determine whether the lifecycle processes adequately protect against circumstances that could result in loss of control over the Wallet Unit, unauthorised use of the Wallet User's digital identity, or continued reliance on a Wallet Unit for which the required security conditions can no longer be assured ([§4.2, p. 16](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04W_Wallet_Onboarding__Lifecycle_Mngt_-_reqs_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=16)). Each requirement's Guidelines include a bullet beginning "A CAB verifies", e.g. [INST-5.1-01](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-04w#inst-5-1-01) ([§5.1, p. 17](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04W_Wallet_Onboarding__Lifecycle_Mngt_-_reqs_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=17)).
- Wallet User: e.g. must be given an explicit choice on creating a Migration Object during activation ([ACT-6.7-01](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-04w#act-6-7-01)) ([§6.7, p. 44](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04W_Wallet_Onboarding__Lifecycle_Mngt_-_reqs_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=44)) and a means to report suspected compromise without authenticating to the Wallet Unit ([MON-13.1-03](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-04w#mon-13-1-03)) ([§13.1, p. 115](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04W_Wallet_Onboarding__Lifecycle_Mngt_-_reqs_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=115)).

## Key points

- Requirements are either applicable to any EUDIW implementation (no marking) or to specific implementations, marked "[CONDITIONAL]" ([§3.4, p. 12](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04W_Wallet_Onboarding__Lifecycle_Mngt_-_reqs_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=12)). 17 requirements carry the [CONDITIONAL] marking (counted).
- Modal verbs are interpreted per clause 23 of the ETSI Drafting Rules ([§3.3, p. 12](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04W_Wallet_Onboarding__Lifecycle_Mngt_-_reqs_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=12)).
- The Wallet Provider is not, as Wallet Provider, subject to CIR (EU) 2015/1502; "high assurance" here expresses the objective that onboarding and lifecycle processes "shall not introduce weaknesses that could materially undermine the assurance associated with the Wallet User's digital identity" ([§4.2, p. 15-16](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04W_Wallet_Onboarding__Lifecycle_Mngt_-_reqs_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=15)).
- The Wallet Provider shall sign the Wallet Instance software package ([INST-5.1-01](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-04w#inst-5-1-01)) and hold the private signing key in an HSM that requires multi-person authorisation for each signing operation and is certified to FIPS 140-3 Level 3, Common Criteria EAL4+, or an equivalent recognised level ([INST-5.1-04](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-04w#inst-5-1-04)) ([§5.1, p. 17-18](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04W_Wallet_Onboarding__Lifecycle_Mngt_-_reqs_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=17)).
- The Wallet Provider shall record every administrative action that modifies the Wallet Instance software package, the code-signing infrastructure or the distribution channel configuration, with operator identity and time, in a tamper-evident log retained for at least 12 months ([INST-5.1-05](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-04w#inst-5-1-05)) ([§5.1, p. 19](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04W_Wallet_Onboarding__Lifecycle_Mngt_-_reqs_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=19)).
- The Wallet Provider shall ensure that the Wallet Instance, at installation and at each subsequent launch, verifies that the OS version and security patch level meet the minimum requirements documented by the Wallet Provider, and refuses to activate Wallet Unit functionality on devices that do not ([INST-5.2-01](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-04w#inst-5-2-01)) ([§5.2, p. 20](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04W_Wallet_Onboarding__Lifecycle_Mngt_-_reqs_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=20)).
- The Wallet Provider shall ensure that all private keys attested in a Key Attestation and all private keys of device-bound PIDs and attestations are generated within the WSCD and never extracted from the WSCD in the clear ([ACT-6.1-02](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-04w#act-6-1-02)) ([§6.1, p. 28](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04W_Wallet_Onboarding__Lifecycle_Mngt_-_reqs_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=28)).
- The Wallet Provider shall manage WIA and Key Attestation validity periods such that a non-revoked Wallet Unit can at all times present a WIA and a Key Attestation each with a remaining validity period of at least 31 days ([ACT-6.2-09](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-04w#act-6-2-09)) ([§6.2, p. 32](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04W_Wallet_Onboarding__Lifecycle_Mngt_-_reqs_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=32)).
- The Wallet Provider shall ensure that at least one storage option offered for the Migration Object is EU based, under the Wallet User's exclusive control and not a location controlled by the Wallet Provider ([ACT-6.7-05](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-04w#act-6-7-05)) ([§6.7, p. 46](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04W_Wallet_Onboarding__Lifecycle_Mngt_-_reqs_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=46)).
- The Wallet Provider shall ensure that the Wallet Unit accepts PID only after the PID Provider has confirmed, through identity proofing at Level of Assurance High as specified in CIR (EU) 2015/1502, that the Wallet User is the natural person to whom the PID data corresponds ([PID-8.2-01](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-04w#pid-8-2-01)) ([§8.2, p. 52](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04W_Wallet_Onboarding__Lifecycle_Mngt_-_reqs_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=52)); before accepting a PID credential, the Wallet Unit shall verify that the PID Provider's signing certificate traces to a trust anchor published by the European Commission in the list of trusted entities applicable to PID Providers ([PID-8.3-01](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-04w#pid-8-3-01)) ([§8.3, p. 55](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04W_Wallet_Onboarding__Lifecycle_Mngt_-_reqs_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=55)).
- A revocation of a Wallet Unit, or establishment of a replacement Wallet Unit, carried out by support staff on behalf of a Wallet User takes effect only after a second member of staff has approved it ([MAINT-9.5-06](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-04w#maint-9-5-06)) ([§9.5, p. 75](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04W_Wallet_Onboarding__Lifecycle_Mngt_-_reqs_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=75)); the maximum time for the second approval is determined as part of the risk assessment and shall be met ([MAINT-9.5-08](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-04w#maint-9-5-08), [MAINT-9.5-09](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-04w#maint-9-5-09)) ([§9.5, p. 76](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04W_Wallet_Onboarding__Lifecycle_Mngt_-_reqs_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=76)).
- Documented periodic reviews of the security controls and procedures governing the Wallet Unit infrastructure at intervals not exceeding twelve months ([MAINT-9.7-02](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-04w#maint-9-7-02)) ([§9.7, p. 81](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04W_Wallet_Onboarding__Lifecycle_Mngt_-_reqs_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=81)); the applicable trusted lists and lists of trusted entities shall be retrieved at intervals not exceeding 24 hours ([MAINT-9.9-01](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-04w#maint-9-9-01)) ([§9.9, p. 82](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04W_Wallet_Onboarding__Lifecycle_Mngt_-_reqs_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=82)).
- Any backup shall be encrypted before being stored or transmitted outside the Wallet Unit, using a password known only to the Wallet User ([MAINT-9.6-02](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-04w#maint-9-6-02)), and shall remain unreadable to the Wallet Provider and to the operator of any storage location offered or used ([MAINT-9.6-04](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-04w#maint-9-6-04)) ([§9.6, p. 77](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04W_Wallet_Onboarding__Lifecycle_Mngt_-_reqs_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=77)).
- The Wallet Provider shall ensure that migration is initiated solely by the Wallet User from the source Wallet Unit, without Wallet Provider authorisation, approval or notification as a precondition ([MIG-10.1-01](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-04w#mig-10-1-01)) ([§10.1, p. 85](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04W_Wallet_Onboarding__Lifecycle_Mngt_-_reqs_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=85)); for recovery to a new device when the source device is unavailable (clause 10.7), identity proofing at the assurance level applicable to initial PID issuance is required before any PID credential is re-issued ([MIG-10.7-02](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-04w#mig-10-7-02)) ([§10.7, p. 94](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04W_Wallet_Onboarding__Lifecycle_Mngt_-_reqs_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=94)).
- Revocation is permanent and irreversible ([§3.1, p. 11](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04W_Wallet_Onboarding__Lifecycle_Mngt_-_reqs_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=11)); a revoked Wallet Unit shall be disabled within a defined maximum timeframe ([REV-12.2-02](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-04w#rev-12-2-02)) ([§12.2, p. 106](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04W_Wallet_Onboarding__Lifecycle_Mngt_-_reqs_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=106)), and the Wallet Provider shall document and enforce that maximum delay ([REV-12.2-03](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-04w#rev-12-2-03)) ([§12.2, p. 107](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04W_Wallet_Onboarding__Lifecycle_Mngt_-_reqs_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=107)); WIA/KA revocation status shall be published within a defined and documented maximum period, in a status list that PID Providers and Attestation Providers can query without authenticated access ([REV-12.3-01](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-04w#rev-12-3-01)) ([§12.3, p. 110](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04W_Wallet_Onboarding__Lifecycle_Mngt_-_reqs_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=110)).
- The Wallet Provider shall treat a notice from the PID Provider of the decease of a Wallet Holder as a request under [REV-12.1-03](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-04w#rev-12-1-03) for permanent revocation of the Wallet Unit ([REV-12.1-08](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-04w#rev-12-1-08)) ([§12.1, p. 105](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04W_Wallet_Onboarding__Lifecycle_Mngt_-_reqs_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=105)).
- Per the [MON-13.1-01](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-04w#mon-13-1-01) guidelines, monitoring draws only on interactions the Wallet Provider receives; relying party interactions and credential presentations do not pass through the Wallet Provider and are not a monitoring input ([§13.1, p. 114](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04W_Wallet_Onboarding__Lifecycle_Mngt_-_reqs_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=114)). The Wallet Provider shall maintain a push channel, independent of the Wallet Instance, for warning the Wallet User when compromise of the Wallet Unit is suspected ([MON-13.4-01](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-04w#mon-13-4-01)) ([§13.4, p. 118](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04W_Wallet_Onboarding__Lifecycle_Mngt_-_reqs_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=118)).
- Suspension and reactivation are not part of the lifecycle model; an untrusted Wallet Unit is revoked and recovery is by activating a new Wallet Unit ([Annex A, p. 120](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04W_Wallet_Onboarding__Lifecycle_Mngt_-_reqs_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=120)).
