## What this document is

NL-SYS-02 is the vocabulary of the NL EUDI Wallet Certification System; its document type is "Vocabulary" ([§cover, p. 2](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-02_Vocabulary_RC1.0_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=2)). Its table of contents lists three parts: “Acronyms and Abbreviations”, “Terms by Category” and “Alphabetical Glossary”; there is no introduction or scope section ([§Table of Contents, p. 4](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-02_Vocabulary_RC1.0_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=4)). The glossary gives for each term a definition, a “Category:” line and a “Source:” line ([§Alphabetical Glossary, p. 9](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-02_Vocabulary_RC1.0_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=9)).

## Scope and object of certification

The document does not define an object of certification itself. The cover states the certification target as "European Digital Identity Wallets and the electronic identification scheme under which they are provided" ([§cover, p. 2](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-02_Vocabulary_RC1.0_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=2)). It defines "Top-level certificate" as a "statement of conformity to European Commission and EDICG in accordance with EU 2024/1183 Article 5d.1. based on the set of certificates, resulted from implementation of all respective certification schemes within PL EUDI Wallet Certification system" ([§Alphabetical Glossary, p. 25](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-02_Vocabulary_RC1.0_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=25)).

## Who it applies to

The document contains no requirements addressed to any role ([§Alphabetical Glossary, p. 9-29](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-02_Vocabulary_RC1.0_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=9)). Its cover lists the audience: primary "System/Scheme Owner, CABs, EUDI Wallet providers, PID Provider"; secondary "EUDI Wallet ecosystem stakeholders" ([§cover, p. 2](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-02_Vocabulary_RC1.0_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=2)). The "Actor" category ("Entities that participate in the EUDI Wallet ecosystem") lists 21 actors: Attestation Provider; Authentic Source; Conformity Assessment Body (CAB); Conformity Assessment Body – Certification Body (CAB-CB); Conformity Assessment Body – Testing Laboratory (ITSEF) (CAB-ITSEF); Device manufacturer; Holder; Identity verification provider (IDVP); Intermediary; National Accreditation Bodies (NAB); Provider of registration certificates; Provider of wallet-relying party access certificates; Public Sector Body; Qualified Electronic Signature Remote Creation Provider; Qualified Trust Service Provider (QTSP); Registrar; Relying Party; User; Verifier; Wallet Provider; Wallet User ([§Terms by Category, p. 6](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-SYS-02_Vocabulary_RC1.0_EUDIW-NL-CS_-_for_publication.pdf#page=6)).
