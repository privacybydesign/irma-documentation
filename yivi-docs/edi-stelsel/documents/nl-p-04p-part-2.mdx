## What this document is

This specification addresses the lifecycle events of the PID after issuance — revocation by the PID Provider, expiry at the end of the validity period, and user-initiated deletion — and establishes security requirements that the PID Provider must implement so that PID lifecycle management meets eIDAS Assurance Level High per CIR (EU) 2015/1502 ([§1.1, p. 5](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04P_PID_Issuance__LifecycleMngt_-reqs-part2_-_RC1.0_for_pub.pdf#page=5)). It is intended as a normative basis for conformity assessment of PID Provider solutions with respect to PID lifecycle management, used by PID Providers for implementation and by CABs for audit and conformity assessment ([§1.1, p. 5](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04P_PID_Issuance__LifecycleMngt_-reqs-part2_-_RC1.0_for_pub.pdf#page=5)). Requirements are risk-based, with a companion risk register maintained as a separate deliverable ([§1.1, p. 5](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04P_PID_Issuance__LifecycleMngt_-reqs-part2_-_RC1.0_for_pub.pdf#page=5)).

## Scope and object of certification

- Covers revocation, expiry and user-initiated deletion of the PID within the EUDI Wallet framework; the PID is treated as an object governed by the ARF state model ([§1.2, p. 5](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04P_PID_Issuance__LifecycleMngt_-reqs-part2_-_RC1.0_for_pub.pdf#page=5)).
- Lifecycle events: PID Revocation (permanent invalidation of a PID by the PID Provider); PID Expiry (natural end of the validity period); PID Deletion (removal of a PID from the Wallet Unit at the initiative of the Wallet User) ([§1.2, p. 6](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04P_PID_Issuance__LifecycleMngt_-reqs-part2_-_RC1.0_for_pub.pdf#page=6)).
- Out of scope: PID initial issuance and renewal (covered in a separate specification); PID Provider organisational, security and policy requirements; PID data model; Authentic Source processes; Wallet Unit or Wallet Instance lifecycle; Relying Party requirements ([§1.2, p. 6](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04P_PID_Issuance__LifecycleMngt_-reqs-part2_-_RC1.0_for_pub.pdf#page=6)).
- "PID" denotes the logical PID unless a technical PID (an individual issued instance) is meant ([§1.1, p. 5](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04P_PID_Issuance__LifecycleMngt_-reqs-part2_-_RC1.0_for_pub.pdf#page=5)).
- The document distinguishes revocation ("permanent and irreversible invalidation of one or more Technical PIDs by the PID Provider"), expiry ("natural end of the validity period of a Technical PID") and deletion ("removal of the PID and associated data and key material from the Wallet Unit at the initiative of the Wallet User") ([§4.1, p. 11](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04P_PID_Issuance__LifecycleMngt_-reqs-part2_-_RC1.0_for_pub.pdf#page=11)).

## Who it applies to

- PID Provider: subject to assurance level High requirements of CIR (EU) 2015/1502 ([§4.2, p. 12](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04P_PID_Issuance__LifecycleMngt_-reqs-part2_-_RC1.0_for_pub.pdf#page=12)); the REV and EXP requirements place obligations on the PID Provider (e.g. [REV-4.1-02](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-04p-part-2#rev-4-1-02) "The PID Provider shall revoke the outstanding Technical PIDs it has issued to a Wallet Unit when a revocation condition applies ..."; [EXP-5.1-01](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-04p-part-2#exp-5-1-01) "The PID Provider shall detect, in its administration, when the validity period of a Technical PID has ended.") ([§5.1, p. 13](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04P_PID_Issuance__LifecycleMngt_-reqs-part2_-_RC1.0_for_pub.pdf#page=13); [§6.1, p. 25](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04P_PID_Issuance__LifecycleMngt_-reqs-part2_-_RC1.0_for_pub.pdf#page=25)).
- Wallet Unit: the DEL requirements are formulated as "The Wallet Unit shall ..." ([DEL-6.1-01](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-04p-part-2#del-6-1-01) to [DEL-6.3-01](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-04p-part-2#del-6-3-01)) ([§7, p. 29-30](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04P_PID_Issuance__LifecycleMngt_-reqs-part2_-_RC1.0_for_pub.pdf#page=29)). The chapter NOTE states there are "no requirements on the PID Provider, only on the capabilities of the Wallet Unit and the on the Wallet Provider" for deletion ([§7, p. 29](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04P_PID_Issuance__LifecycleMngt_-reqs-part2_-_RC1.0_for_pub.pdf#page=29)).
- Wallet Providers: receive notification from the PID Provider of a person's decease ([REV-5.1-08](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-04p-part-2#rev-5-1-08)) ([§5.1, p. 15](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04P_PID_Issuance__LifecycleMngt_-reqs-part2_-_RC1.0_for_pub.pdf#page=15)), forwarded revocation requests ([REV-5.2-10](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-04p-part-2#rev-5-2-10)) and forwarded loss/theft reports ([REV-5.2-12](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-04p-part-2#rev-5-2-12)) ([§5.2, p. 19](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04P_PID_Issuance__LifecycleMngt_-reqs-part2_-_RC1.0_for_pub.pdf#page=19)).
- The person / Wallet User: can view an overview of every Wallet Unit holding a PID in their name after authentication at assurance level substantial or high, not via a Wallet Unit ([REV-5.2-08](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-04p-part-2#rev-5-2-08)); can report loss, theft or compromise without authenticating through a Wallet Unit ([REV-5.2-11](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-04p-part-2#rev-5-2-11)) ([§5.2, p. 18-19](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04P_PID_Issuance__LifecycleMngt_-reqs-part2_-_RC1.0_for_pub.pdf#page=18)).
- CAB: shall assess the individual requirements and their combined effectiveness across the post-issuance lifecycle, including whether a high-attack-potential attacker could circumvent the intended outcome ([§4.1, p. 11](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04P_PID_Issuance__LifecycleMngt_-reqs-part2_-_RC1.0_for_pub.pdf#page=11)); shall assess whether the processes preserve assurance level High and continued sole control ([§4.2, p. 12](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04P_PID_Issuance__LifecycleMngt_-reqs-part2_-_RC1.0_for_pub.pdf#page=12)).

## Key points

- Revocation triggers: reliable information that the Wallet Unit or its device is no longer under the legitimate user's control ([REV-4.1-01](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-04p-part-2#rev-4-1-01)); revocation conditions including loss of eligibility, decease, confirmed fraud or a substantiated request of the legitimate user ([REV-4.1-02](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-04p-part-2#rev-4-1-02)); revocation of the Wallet Unit ([REV-4.1-05](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-04p-part-2#rev-4-1-05)) ([§5.1, p. 13-14](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04P_PID_Issuance__LifecycleMngt_-reqs-part2_-_RC1.0_for_pub.pdf#page=13)).
- An indication of a revocation condition is acted upon only after its reliability is established through a defined verification procedure ([REV-4.1-03](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-04p-part-2#rev-4-1-03)) ([§5.1, p. 13](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04P_PID_Issuance__LifecycleMngt_-reqs-part2_-_RC1.0_for_pub.pdf#page=13)).
- On revocation, every outstanding Technical PID of the Logical PID issued to the Wallet Unit is revoked, including batch-replenished ones ([REV-4.1-04](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-04p-part-2#rev-4-1-04)), and the Administrative Validity Period is ended ([REV-4.1-06](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-04p-part-2#rev-4-1-06)) ([§5.1, p. 14-15](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04P_PID_Issuance__LifecycleMngt_-reqs-part2_-_RC1.0_for_pub.pdf#page=14)).
- [CONDITIONAL] Where the authentic source provides event-driven notification of decease or loss of eligibility, the PID Provider subscribes to it for each person with an active Logical PID ([REV-5.1-07](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-04p-part-2#rev-5-1-07)) ([§5.1, p. 15](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04P_PID_Issuance__LifecycleMngt_-reqs-part2_-_RC1.0_for_pub.pdf#page=15)).
- The maximum revocation time is set in the risk assessment and shall not exceed the maximum time for revoking an EAA in ETSI TS 119 471 clause 4.2.5.1 ([REV-4.2-01](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-04p-part-2#rev-4-2-01), [REV-4.2-02](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-04p-part-2#rev-4-2-02)); revocation is completed within it ([REV-4.2-03](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-04p-part-2#rev-4-2-03)) ([§5.2, p. 16](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04P_PID_Issuance__LifecycleMngt_-reqs-part2_-_RC1.0_for_pub.pdf#page=16)).
- A revoked Technical PID shall not be reinstated ([REV-4.2-05](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-04p-part-2#rev-4-2-05)) ([§5.2, p. 17](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04P_PID_Issuance__LifecycleMngt_-reqs-part2_-_RC1.0_for_pub.pdf#page=17)).
- The PID Provider periodically verifies the validity status of each Wallet Unit holding non-terminal Technical PIDs, at a risk-assessed interval ([REV-4.2-06](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-04p-part-2#rev-4-2-06), [REV-4.2-07](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-04p-part-2#rev-4-2-07)) ([§5.2, p. 17-18](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04P_PID_Issuance__LifecycleMngt_-reqs-part2_-_RC1.0_for_pub.pdf#page=17)).
- Employee-initiated revocation takes effect only after approval by a second employee, both approvals recorded in a tamper-evident log, within a risk-assessed maximum time ([REV-5.2-14](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-04p-part-2#rev-5-2-14) to [REV-5.2-16](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-04p-part-2#rev-5-2-16)) ([§5.2, p. 20](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04P_PID_Issuance__LifecycleMngt_-reqs-part2_-_RC1.0_for_pub.pdf#page=20)).
- Each Technical PID includes the reference to its status mechanism, as required by ETSI TS 119 471 and the attestation format profile ETSI TS 119 472-1 ([REV-4.3-01](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-04p-part-2#rev-4-3-01)), and the identifier or index that distinguishes it within that mechanism, as required by ETSI TS 119 471 ([REV-4.3-02](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-04p-part-2#rev-4-3-02)) ([§5.3, p. 20-21](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04P_PID_Issuance__LifecycleMngt_-reqs-part2_-_RC1.0_for_pub.pdf#page=20)).
- Revocation-status information need not be maintained for a Technical PID with a validity period of less than 24 hours, if stated in the PID provision policy ([REV-4.3-04](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-04p-part-2#rev-4-3-04)) ([§5.3, p. 21](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04P_PID_Issuance__LifecycleMngt_-reqs-part2_-_RC1.0_for_pub.pdf#page=21)).
- The maximum delay to publish revoked status is risk-assessed and bounded by ETSI TS 119 471 clause 4.2.5.1 ([REV-4.3-05](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-04p-part-2#rev-4-3-05) to [REV-4.3-07](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-04p-part-2#rev-4-3-07)) ([§5.3, p. 22](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04P_PID_Issuance__LifecycleMngt_-reqs-part2_-_RC1.0_for_pub.pdf#page=22)).
- The Wallet User is notified of revocation through the government electronic mailbox (MijnOverheid Berichtenbox) and additionally through a registered contact method; the notification identifies the PID, the Wallet Provider and the Wallet Unit issuance date ([REV-4.4-01](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-04p-part-2#rev-4-4-01), -02, -04, [REV-5.4-05](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-04p-part-2#rev-5-4-05), -06) ([§5.4, p. 23-24](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04P_PID_Issuance__LifecycleMngt_-reqs-part2_-_RC1.0_for_pub.pdf#page=23)).
- Expiry: the PID Provider detects the end of validity and stops treating the Technical PID as valid ([EXP-5.1-01](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-04p-part-2#exp-5-1-01), -02); it notifies the Wallet User before the Administrative Validity Period ends, with risk-assessed advance time ([EXP-5.1-03](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-04p-part-2#exp-5-1-03), -04) ([§6.1, p. 25-26](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04P_PID_Issuance__LifecycleMngt_-reqs-part2_-_RC1.0_for_pub.pdf#page=25)).
- A Technical PID is not revoked solely because its validity period has ended, and revocation-status information need not be maintained after that ([EXP-5.2-01](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-04p-part-2#exp-5-2-01), [EXP-5.2-02](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-04p-part-2#exp-5-2-02)) ([§6.2, p. 26](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04P_PID_Issuance__LifecycleMngt_-reqs-part2_-_RC1.0_for_pub.pdf#page=26)).
- Expired Technical PIDs are removed per a defined cleanup procedure, with a risk-assessed retention period; audit and status records are kept until their retention period ends ([EXP-5.4-01](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-04p-part-2#exp-5-4-01) to -03) ([§6.4, p. 27-28](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04P_PID_Issuance__LifecycleMngt_-reqs-part2_-_RC1.0_for_pub.pdf#page=27)).
- Deletion: the Wallet Unit allows user-initiated deletion with confirmation, erases attribute data and key material irrecoverably, does not notify the PID Provider, and informs the Wallet User that deletion does not revoke the PID ([DEL-6.1-01](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-04p-part-2#del-6-1-01) to [DEL-6.3-01](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-04p-part-2#del-6-3-01)) ([§7.1-7.3, p. 29-30](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04P_PID_Issuance__LifecycleMngt_-reqs-part2_-_RC1.0_for_pub.pdf#page=29)). The NOTE cites ARF requirement PAD_03 as prohibiting the Wallet Unit from notifying the PID Provider of a deletion ([§7, p. 29](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-04P_PID_Issuance__LifecycleMngt_-reqs-part2_-_RC1.0_for_pub.pdf#page=29)).
