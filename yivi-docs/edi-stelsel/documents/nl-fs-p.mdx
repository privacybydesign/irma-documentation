## What this document is

NL-FS-P specifies the functional certification scope for a person identification data (PID) issuance service within the common EUDI Wallet Certification System and the corresponding requirements for functional conformance, and provides a corresponding Implementation Conformance Statement (ICS) ([§1, p. 5](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS-P_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_-_PID_Issuance_Service_Conformance_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=5)). It identifies the documents forming the legal basis for certification ([§1, p. 5](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS-P_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_-_PID_Issuance_Service_Conformance_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=5)). It is one of the two principal scope documents of the functional certification scheme (the other is [NL-FS-W](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-fs-w)) and operates within the framework, methodology and evaluation rules of [NL-FS](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-fs) ([§1, p. 5](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS-P_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_-_PID_Issuance_Service_Conformance_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=5)).

## Scope and object of certification

- The object of certification is "a PID issuance service provided by a PID provider" ([§5, p. 9](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS-P_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_-_PID_Issuance_Service_Conformance_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=9)).
- The certification scope comprises: (a) the conformity of the functional behaviour of the service observable at its interface towards the wallet unit, by which person identification data is issued; (b) the conformity of the person identification data issued by the service ([§5, p. 9](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS-P_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_-_PID_Issuance_Service_Conformance_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=9)).
- Legal derivation: Regulation (EU) No 910/2014 as amended by Regulation (EU) 2024/1183, in particular Article 5a and Article 5c(1); under Article 5c(1) the scope comprises the requirements of Article 5a(4), (5) and (8) and, where applicable, the standards and technical specifications referred to in Article 5a(24) ([§2, p. 6](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS-P_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_-_PID_Issuance_Service_Conformance_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=6)).

## Who it applies to

- Conformity assessment bodies: the document is intended for them ([§1, p. 5](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS-P_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_-_PID_Issuance_Service_Conformance_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=5)). During certification the certification body shall request the documentation for any Recommended feature that is not implemented ([§6.1, p. 10](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS-P_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_-_PID_Issuance_Service_Conformance_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=10)).
- PID providers preparing a PID issuance service for certification: the document is intended for them ([§1, p. 5](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS-P_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_-_PID_Issuance_Service_Conformance_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=5)). PID providers seeking functional certification "shall complete this ICS" ([§7.1, p. 12](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS-P_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_-_PID_Issuance_Service_Conformance_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=12)) and shall declare whether the PID issuance service implements each listed feature ([Annex A.3, p. 13](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS-P_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_-_PID_Issuance_Service_Conformance_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=13)). For a Recommended feature not implemented, the PID provider shall understand and weigh the implications and document these considerations ([§6.1, p. 10](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS-P_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_-_PID_Issuance_Service_Conformance_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=10)). The PID provider shall declare which optional PID attributes and metadata it supports using an ICS that is part of the test case specification for PID contents ([Annex A.3 Note, p. 13](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS-P_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_-_PID_Issuance_Service_Conformance_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=13)).

## Key points

- Chapter 6 lists the requirements regarding features of PID issuance services mandated in the legal instruments; these "enable modularity of the certification" and allow a PID provider to indicate which legally mandated features are implemented ([§6.1, p. 10](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS-P_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_-_PID_Issuance_Service_Conformance_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=10)).
- Requirements are categorised by the CIR in which the feature is (primarily) discussed; the rightmost column gives the conformance level ([§6.1, p. 10](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS-P_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_-_PID_Issuance_Service_Conformance_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=10)).
- Conformance levels Optional, Recommended, Required "shall be interpreted as specified in RFC 2119" ([§6.1, p. 10](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS-P_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_-_PID_Issuance_Service_Conformance_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=10)).
- Recommended: "there may exist valid reasons in particular circumstances for a PID provider to not implement such a feature in their PID issuance service, but the PID provider shall understand and carefully weigh the full implications before doing so and shall document these considerations"; the certification body shall request this documentation for any recommended feature not implemented ([§6.1, p. 10](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS-P_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_-_PID_Issuance_Service_Conformance_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=10)).
- "Where a PID issuance service implements a feature, it shall comply with all related functional requirements in the legal basis and in the corresponding standards mentioned therein" ([§6.1, p. 10](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS-P_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_-_PID_Issuance_Service_Conformance_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=10)).
- Note in the document: "In this version of this document, the lists of requirements may not be fully complete. Additionally, indicated conformance levels are subject to change" ([§6.1, p. 10](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS-P_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_-_PID_Issuance_Service_Conformance_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=10)).
- No requirements are listed from CIR 2024/2979 ("None.") ([§6.2, p. 10](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS-P_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_-_PID_Issuance_Service_Conformance_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=10); [Annex A.3.1, p. 13](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS-P_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_-_PID_Issuance_Service_Conformance_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=13)).
- Requirements: #1 PID issuance using OpenID4VCI — Recommended; #2 PID issuance using the NL-specific issuance specification — Required (legal reference "-") ([§6.3, p. 10](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS-P_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_-_PID_Issuance_Service_Conformance_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=10)); #3 PIDs in mdoc format — Required; #4 PIDs in SD-JWT VC format — Required ([§6.4, p. 11](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS-P_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_-_PID_Issuance_Service_Conformance_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=11)).
- The ICS declared implementation choices (Annex A.3) reflect the conformance requirements of Chapter 6 ([§7.1, p. 12](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS-P_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_-_PID_Issuance_Service_Conformance_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=12)), with a "Declaration (Yes / No)" column ([Annex A.3.2–A.3.3, p. 13–14](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS-P_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_-_PID_Issuance_Service_Conformance_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=13)).
- The PID provider does not declare in this ICS which optional PID attributes and metadata it supports, although PID contents are specified in CIR 2024/2977; it shall declare this using an ICS that is part of the test case specification for PID contents (see [NL-FS](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-fs) §6.3) ([Annex A.3 Note, p. 13](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS-P_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_-_PID_Issuance_Service_Conformance_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=13)).
- Annex A.1 identification fields: PID issuance service name; Version ([Annex A.1, p. 13](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS-P_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_-_PID_Issuance_Service_Conformance_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=13)).
- Annex A.2 identification fields: PID provider name; full address; contact person name, title; contact phone number; contact e-mail address ([Annex A.2, p. 13](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS-P_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_-_PID_Issuance_Service_Conformance_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=13)).
