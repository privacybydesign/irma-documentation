## What this document is

NL-P-01 specifies common organisational, technical, operational, security and resilience requirements for Wallet Providers and PID Providers participating in the NL EUDI Wallet Certification Scheme ("Stelsel") ([§1.1, p. 5](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-01_Wallet_and_PID_Provider_Requirements_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=5)). Its structure follows ETSI EN 319 401 V3.2.1, with references to a Trust Service Provider or trust service adapted to the Wallet Provider, PID Provider and provider service context; the document does not claim that Wallet Providers or PID Providers are trust service providers solely because this structure is used ([§1.1, p. 5](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-01_Wallet_and_PID_Provider_Requirements_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=5)). The requirements constitute subsidiary normative requirements for the Wallet Provider Certification Scheme ([NL-P-W](/edi-stelsel/documents/nl-p-w)) and the PID Provider Certification Scheme ([NL-P-P](/edi-stelsel/documents/nl-p-p)) ([§1.2, p. 5](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-01_Wallet_and_PID_Provider_Requirements_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=5)).

## Scope and object of certification

- The certified scope includes the provider organisation and the people, processes, information, infrastructure, cloud and hosting services, cryptographic services, interfaces, suppliers and operational controls used to provide wallet or PID services ([§1.2, p. 5](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-01_Wallet_and_PID_Provider_Requirements_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=5)).
- For the Wallet Provider this includes wallet onboarding, activation and wallet-unit lifecycle operations; for the PID Provider, identity proofing, PID issuance, binding, status, suspension, revocation and lifecycle operations ([§1.2, p. 5](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-01_Wallet_and_PID_Provider_Requirements_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=5)).
- Wallet Provider and PID Provider certifications are separate subordinate certifications; for a Wallet Provider these certificates form part of the assurance evidence for the top-level certification and national admission decision ([§1.2, p. 5](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-01_Wallet_and_PID_Provider_Requirements_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=5)).
- The document does not define the top-level certification decision, admission conditions, product-functional certification or wallet-solution cybersecurity certification ([§1.2, p. 5](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-01_Wallet_and_PID_Provider_Requirements_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=5)).
- Provider conformity is assessed as service certification under the NL EUDI Wallet Certification System ([§4.1, p. 9](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-01_Wallet_and_PID_Provider_Requirements_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=9)).
- "Certified scope" is defined as the provider role, services, locations, systems, processes, interfaces and outsourced activities covered by the provider certificate ([§3.1, p. 8](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-01_Wallet_and_PID_Provider_Requirements_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=8)).

## Who it applies to

- Wallet Provider: "the legal entity responsible for providing and operating the EUDI Wallet service and for the wallet-provider obligations allocated by [NL-SYS-01](/edi-stelsel/documents/nl-sys-01) and [NL-P-W](/edi-stelsel/documents/nl-p-w)" ([§3.1, p. 8](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-01_Wallet_and_PID_Provider_Requirements_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=8)). It remains responsible for the secure provision and operation of wallet-related services and for maintaining valid assurance evidence for the wallet solution in scope ([§4.1, p. 9](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-01_Wallet_and_PID_Provider_Requirements_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=9)).
- PID Provider: "the legal entity responsible for identity proofing and issuing and managing PID for EUDI Wallet Units, including the PID-provider obligations allocated by [NL-SYS-01](/edi-stelsel/documents/nl-sys-01) and [NL-P-P](/edi-stelsel/documents/nl-p-p)" ([§3.1, p. 8](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-01_Wallet_and_PID_Provider_Requirements_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=8)). It remains responsible for controlled PID issuance, status and lifecycle management, and the supporting trust and fraud-management services within its certified scope ([§4.1, p. 9](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-01_Wallet_and_PID_Provider_Requirements_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=9)).
- "provider": a Wallet Provider, a PID Provider, or both, according to the applicability of a requirement ([§3.1, p. 8](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-01_Wallet_and_PID_Provider_Requirements_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=8)).
- CAB: "shall be given sufficient architecture, service-decomposition and responsibility-allocation information to validate applicability decisions" ([REQ-4.2-03](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-01#req-4-2-03)) ([§4.2, p. 9](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-01_Wallet_and_PID_Provider_Requirements_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=9)); incidents and vulnerabilities that may affect certified conformity shall be reported to the CAB ([REQ-7.9-04](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-01#req-7-9-04)) ([§7.9, p. 20](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-01_Wallet_and_PID_Provider_Requirements_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=20)); the Provider shall ensure the CAB and competent authorities can obtain necessary evidence from outsourced activities ([REQ-7.14-05](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-01#req-7-14-05)) ([§7.14, p. 24](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-01_Wallet_and_PID_Provider_Requirements_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=24)).
- Scheme Owner: receives incident and vulnerability reports ([REQ-7.9-04](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-01#req-7-9-04)) ([§7.9, p. 20](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-01_Wallet_and_PID_Provider_Requirements_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=20)); the termination plan addresses cooperation with the Scheme Owner and CAB ([REQ-7.12-02](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-01#req-7-12-02)) ([§7.12, p. 23](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-01_Wallet_and_PID_Provider_Requirements_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=23)).
- Applicant: shall identify the competent authority, applicable reporting obligations and any sector-specific cybersecurity requirements relevant to its certified services ([§2.1, p. 6](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-01_Wallet_and_PID_Provider_Requirements_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=6)).

## Key points

- Unless marked role-specific or conditional, each requirement applies to both provider roles and to all systems and outsourced services within their certified scope ([§4, p. 9](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-01_Wallet_and_PID_Provider_Requirements_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=9)).
- "Shall" identifies a mandatory certification requirement; keywords are interpreted per ETSI drafting rules; requirement identifiers are stable references for evaluation plans, evidence records, findings and certificates ([§3, p. 8](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-01_Wallet_and_PID_Provider_Requirements_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=8)).
- The Provider shall document, for every conditional or role-specific requirement, whether it applies and the factual basis ([REQ-4.2-01](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-01#req-4-2-01)); non-applicability shall not transfer, waive or obscure a responsibility ([REQ-4.2-02](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-01#req-4-2-02)) ([§4.2, p. 9](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-01_Wallet_and_PID_Provider_Requirements_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=9)).
- The Provider shall map its risk assessment to the system risk register maintained under [NL-SYS-04](/edi-stelsel/documents/nl-sys-04) ([REQ-5-04](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-01#req-5-04)) ([§5, p. 10](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-01_Wallet_and_PID_Provider_Requirements_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=10)).
- The risk assessment shall address confidentiality, integrity, availability, authenticity, privacy and sole user control ([REQ-5-03](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-01#req-5-03)) and interfaces between Wallet Provider, PID Provider, wallet solution, WSCA/WSCD, trust services, access-certificate and registration-certificate services ([REQ-5-05](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-01#req-5-05)) ([§5, p. 10](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-01_Wallet_and_PID_Provider_Requirements_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=10)).
- Risks that may affect certificate validity, subordinate assurance evidence, national admission conditions or another provider shall be reported per [NL-SYS-01](/edi-stelsel/documents/nl-sys-01) and the applicable provider scheme ([REQ-5-08](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-01#req-5-08)) ([§5, p. 11](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-01_Wallet_and_PID_Provider_Requirements_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=11)).
- The Provider shall maintain a Provider Practice Statement (the implementation of ETSI EN 319 401's Trust Service Practice Statement), approved by accountable management, version-controlled and reviewed at least annually and after material change ([REQ-6.1-01](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-01#req-6-1-01), [REQ-6.1-04](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-01#req-6-1-04)) ([§6, p. 12](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-01_Wallet_and_PID_Provider_Requirements_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=12); [§6.1, p. 12](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-01_Wallet_and_PID_Provider_Requirements_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=12)).
- Terms and conditions shall be made available before service use ([REQ-6.2-01](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-01#req-6-2-01)); the Wallet Provider explains eligibility, onboarding, activation, recovery, suspension, termination and loss/compromise ([REQ-6.2-03](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-01#req-6-2-03)); the PID Provider explains PID issuance, validity, status, revocation, correction and user redress ([REQ-6.2-04](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-01#req-6-2-04)) ([§6.2, p. 12-13](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-01_Wallet_and_PID_Provider_Requirements_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=12)).
- Incompatible duties shall be segregated, including development and production administration and key activation and key use ([REQ-7.1-04](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-01#req-7-1-04)) ([§7.1, p. 14](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-01_Wallet_and_PID_Provider_Requirements_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=14)).
- Multi-factor authentication shall protect remote access, privileged access and access to security-critical systems ([REQ-7.4-06](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-01#req-7-4-06)) ([§7.4, p. 16](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-01_Wallet_and_PID_Provider_Requirements_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=16)).
- The PID Provider shall protect PID signing or sealing keys and associated status-service keys ([REQ-7.5-05](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-01#req-7-5-05)) ([§7.5, p. 17](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-01_Wallet_and_PID_Provider_Requirements_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=17)).
- Development, test, acceptance and production environments shall be appropriately separated ([REQ-7.7-03](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-01#req-7-7-03)) ([§7.7, p. 18](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-01_Wallet_and_PID_Provider_Requirements_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=18)).
- Incidents and vulnerabilities that may affect certified conformity, users, another provider or the trust framework shall be reported to the CAB, Scheme Owner and competent authorities within applicable deadlines ([REQ-7.9-04](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-01#req-7-9-04)) ([§7.9, p. 20](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-01_Wallet_and_PID_Provider_Requirements_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=20)); each Provider shall additionally assess effects on the counterpart provider and the national trust framework ([§7.9, p. 20](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-01_Wallet_and_PID_Provider_Requirements_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=20)).
- The PID Provider shall assess systemic impact where an incident may affect multiple Wallet Providers or issued PIDs ([REQ-7.9-08](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-01#req-7-9-08)) ([§7.9, p. 21](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-01_Wallet_and_PID_Provider_Requirements_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=21)).
- The Provider shall maintain traceability from each requirement to implemented control, evidence source, owner, assessment result and open finding ([REQ-7.10-04](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-01#req-7-10-04)) ([§7.10, p. 21](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-01_Wallet_and_PID_Provider_Requirements_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=21)).
- Business impact analysis shall define maximum tolerable disruption, recovery priorities, RTOs and RPOs ([REQ-7.11-02](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-01#req-7-11-02)); continuity exercises shall include compromise or unavailability of a counterpart Wallet Provider or PID Provider ([REQ-7.11-06](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-01#req-7-11-06)) ([§7.11, p. 22](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-01_Wallet_and_PID_Provider_Requirements_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=22)).
- The Provider shall maintain a termination plan ([REQ-7.12-01](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-01#req-7-12-01)); the Wallet Provider plan addresses wallet suspension or migration, user access to records, and consequences for active wallet units ([REQ-7.12-03](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-01#req-7-12-03)); the PID Provider plan addresses continuity of PID validity status, revocation information, evidence retention and effects on all dependent wallets ([REQ-7.12-04](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-01#req-7-12-04)) ([§7.12, p. 22-23](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-01_Wallet_and_PID_Provider_Requirements_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=22)).
- The Provider shall conduct planned internal audits covering all requirements of the document and the applicable provider scheme ([REQ-7.13-02](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-01#req-7-13-02)) and notify material changes affecting the certified object, assurance evidence or certificate scope before implementation where the scheme requires ([REQ-7.13-05](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-01#req-7-13-05)) ([§7.13, p. 23-24](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-01_Wallet_and_PID_Provider_Requirements_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=23)).
- The Provider remains accountable for outsourced activities ([REQ-7.14-05](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-01#req-7-14-05)) and shall maintain exit and substitution plans for critical suppliers ([REQ-7.14-07](/edi-stelsel/requirements/nl-p-01#req-7-14-07)) ([§7.14, p. 24-25](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-01_Wallet_and_PID_Provider_Requirements_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=24)).
- Where national transposition or sector-specific rules impose more specific or stricter requirements, those prevail ([§2.1, p. 6](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-01_Wallet_and_PID_Provider_Requirements_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=6)).
- Annexes A and B are informative. Annex A is an aid to assessment that does not replace the legal text, and its mapping does not establish legal compliance by itself ([Annex A, A.1-A.2, p. 26](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-01_Wallet_and_PID_Provider_Requirements_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=26)). Annex B is an assessment aid that neither determines whether a Provider falls within CIR 2024/2690 nor replaces the legal text, competent-authority supervision or a required conformity assessment ([Annex B, B.1, p. 28](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-01_Wallet_and_PID_Provider_Requirements_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=28)).
- For CIR 2024/2690, the Provider and CAB "should maintain one traceability matrix" linking each applicable provision to requirement identifiers, controls, owners and evidence ([Annex B, B.1, p. 28](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-P-01_Wallet_and_PID_Provider_Requirements_-_RC1.0_-_for_pub.pdf#page=28)).
