## What this document is

NL-FS specifies the certification scheme for functional conformity of EUDI Wallet components within the Dutch EUDI Wallet Certification System, and establishes the evaluation methodology and conformity assessment procedures that accredited certification bodies shall apply ([§1.1, p. 6](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_Scheme_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=6)). It is the scheme and framework that governs the application of all other NL-FS.x documents ([§5.1.2, p. 11](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_Scheme_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=11)). It does not specify any test cases and does not define the certification scope for individual objects of certification; those scopes and requirements are in [NL-FS-W](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-fs-w) (wallet solutions) and [NL-FS-P](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-fs-p) (PID issuance services) ([§1.3, p. 7](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_Scheme_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=7)).

## Scope and object of certification

- The components covered are a wallet solution and a PID issuance service ([§1.1, p. 6](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_Scheme_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=6)).
- The object of certification is "the functional behaviour of the wallet solution or the PID issuance service — namely, that it correctly implements the protocols, interfaces, data formats, and other functional requirements laid down in the applicable implementing acts and in the technical specifications referenced therein" ([§1.1, p. 6](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_Scheme_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=6)).
- Entities subject to functional certification: (a) wallet providers, in respect of their wallet solution; (b) PID providers, in respect of their PID issuance services ([§1.1, p. 6](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_Scheme_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=6)).
- Rationale for including the PID issuance service: CIR 2024/2981 Article 3(3)(c) states that the object of certification includes the processes supporting provision and operation of a wallet solution, including user onboarding, and covers electronic identification means management, which under Implementing Regulation (EU) 2015/1502 includes issuance, delivery and activation of the eID means; "This implies that PID issuance is part of the object of certification" ([§1.1 Note, p. 6](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_Scheme_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=6)).
- Per platform: "Where a wallet solution includes wallet units for multiple OS versions (e.g. iOS and Android), the certification body shall treat these wallet units as separate objects of certification", because "these wallet units are, in fact, different mobile applications, having different code bases" ([§9.1.1, p. 21](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_Scheme_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=21)).
- Evaluation scope for a wallet solution: functional behaviour at its external interfaces towards users, PID providers, attestation providers, relying parties, and infrastructure components such as Trusted Lists and revocation services ([§6.2, p. 14](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_Scheme_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=14)).
- Evaluation scope for a PID issuance service: functional behaviour at its external interface towards a wallet solution; the conformity of the PID it issues (attributes, data model, encodings) is evaluated against the formats required by the applicable implementing acts, in particular CIR (EU) 2024/2977 ([§6.2, p. 14](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_Scheme_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=14)).
- Out of scope: cybersecurity certification of the wallet solution or PID issuance service (subject of the NL-CS series), and certification of wallet providers in conjunction with the eID scheme (covered by [NL-P-W](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-p-w) and [NL-TL](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-tl)) ([§1.2, p. 6](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_Scheme_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=6)).

## Who it applies to

- Certification body: responsible for the conformity assessment in accordance with ISO/IEC 17065 and [NL-SYS-01](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-sys-01); shall be accredited per the latest version of ISO/IEC 17065 for the scope of this scheme by a national accreditation body appointed pursuant to Regulation (EC) No 765/2008 ([§6.1, p. 13](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_Scheme_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=13)). It derives test objectives and test cases (directly or by reference to an existing test standard such as the FCAF), develops or references the ICS templates ([§6.1(b), p. 13](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_Scheme_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=13)), preferably implements test cases in an automated conformance test tool ([§6.1(c), p. 14](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_Scheme_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=14)), guides the developer in completing the ICSs ([§6.1(d), p. 14](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_Scheme_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=14)), executes or has executed the applicable test cases ([§6.1(e), p. 14](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_Scheme_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=14)), evaluates the evidence ([§6.1(f), p. 14](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_Scheme_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=14)), qualifies test tools ([§9.1.2, p. 21](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_Scheme_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=21)), creates a traceability matrix ([§7.2.3, p. 18](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_Scheme_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=18)), sources supporting systems such as Trusted Lists ([§9.1.1, p. 21](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_Scheme_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=21)), and issues certificates ([§8.4, p. 20](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_Scheme_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=20)). It shall be competent to develop or reference test objectives and test cases ([§7.1, p. 17](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_Scheme_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=17)).
- Outsourced partner / testing laboratory: the certification body may execute tests "directly or through an outsourced partner"; where it uses an outsourced partner, "the partner shall be an ISO/IEC 17025-accredited testing laboratory" ([§6.1(e), p. 14](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_Scheme_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=14)). Where the certification body did not execute the tests itself, the testing laboratory (or the developer) provides the evidence listed in §6.4 ([§6.4, p. 16](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_Scheme_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=16)).
- Developer of the object of certification: declares implemented features by filling out the ICS template of [NL-FS-W](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-fs-w) or [NL-FS-P](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-fs-p) and the ICS templates of each supported technical specification ([§6.1(d), p. 14](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_Scheme_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=14); [§6.3, p. 15](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_Scheme_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=15)). It may execute tests itself, using a testing tool qualified by the certification body, and submits logs and verdicts per test case for review ([§9.1.3, p. 21](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_Scheme_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=21)).
- Wallet providers and PID providers: the entities subject to functional certification ([§1.1, p. 6](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_Scheme_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=6)); they rely on NL-FS, and on [NL-FS-W](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-fs-w) or [NL-FS-P](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-fs-p) respectively ([§5.1.2, p. 11](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_Scheme_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=11)).
- Certification bodies at [NL-P-W](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-p-w) / [NL-P-P](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-p-p) level: verify validity and scope of NL-FS and NL-CS certificates and do not re-evaluate requirements already covered by them ([§5.3, p. 12](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_Scheme_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=12)).

## Key points

- Legal basis: Regulation (EU) No 910/2014 as amended by Regulation (EU) 2024/1183 (in particular Article 5a), CIR 2024/2977, CIR 2024/2979, CIR 2024/2981 (in particular Article 3 and Annex III) and CIR 2024/2982 ([§2, p. 8](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_Scheme_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=8)). Annex III of CIR 2024/2981 requires conformity assessment of functional requirements defined in CIR 2024/2977, 2024/2979 and 2024/2982 ([§2, p. 8](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_Scheme_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=8)).
- A certification under NL-FS is a product certification; the NL-CS series is the parallel product certification addressing the cybersecurity of the wallet solution; "The NL-FS and NL-CS series are assessed and certified independently of each other; neither replaces the other" ([§5.3, p. 12](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_Scheme_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=12)).
- Scheme type: Type 6 as defined in ISO/IEC 17067 (initial testing and evaluation, surveillance, periodic re-evaluation); common Type 6 elements are specified in [NL-SYS-01](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-sys-01), in accordance with Article 4(3)(a) of CIR (EU) 2024/2981 ([§5.4, p. 12](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_Scheme_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=12)).
- An NL-FS certificate is "internal assurance information within the certification hierarchy" and does not of itself confer the right to use the EU Digital Identity Wallet Trust Mark ([§5.5, p. 12](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_Scheme_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=12)).
- Functional requirements "originate strictly from the regulatory sources (the eIDAS Regulation and its implementing acts) only" ([§7.1, p. 17](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_Scheme_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=17)).
- The scheme does not prescribe a set of test objectives and test cases, but recognises that the European Commission's Functional Conformance Assessment Framework (FCAF) "may provide a suitable reference set ... in the future" ([§7.1, p. 17](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_Scheme_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=17)). Referencing the FCAF is not mandatory ([§9.2, p. 22](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_Scheme_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=22)).
- If the certification body references the FCAF or any other external test case specification, it shall review correctness and coverage, review suitability for the object of certification, report gaps in coverage or bugs to the organisation maintaining the test case specification and, where corrections are not made (the text reads "(I time)"), develop additional test cases, and record the version referenced ([§9.2, p. 22](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_Scheme_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=22)).
- Example of an existing test case specification with its own ICS: ISO/IEC 18013-6 (for ISO/IEC 18013-5 proximity presentations); the FCAF ICS is named for SD-JWT VC and OpenID4VP ([§6.3, p. 15](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_Scheme_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=15)).
- Test objectives are natural-language statements derived line-by-line from functional requirements, granular and single-purpose ([§7.1, p. 17](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_Scheme_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=17)). Test cases shall include positive and negative cases and should be atomic ([§7.1, p. 17](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_Scheme_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=17)); they treat the object of certification as a "black box" ([§7.2.1, p. 18](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_Scheme_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=18)).
- Coverage: the certification body shall create or reference test cases that test positive and negative scenarios for each functional requirement, and test cases that exercise different usage scenarios; for example, for a remote attestation presentation, test cases should cover different EAA types and formats ([§7.2.2, p. 18](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_Scheme_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=18)). A traceability matrix shall show full coverage of in-scope functional requirements ([§7.2.3, p. 18](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_Scheme_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=18)).
- Testing shall be conducted in a controlled environment (the "test framework") that ensures testing is impartial and reproducible; testing of wallet solutions shall be performed using clearly defined test devices, operating systems and configurations, in line with the developer's completed ICS ([§9.1.1, p. 21](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_Scheme_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=21)).
- Test tools shall be able to provide valid and invalid inputs and receive responses, execute the test case specification(s), and log a full trace of messages; where used, the certification body qualifies them as fit for purpose ([§9.1.2, p. 21](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_Scheme_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=21)).
- Test execution "should be performed by the certification body (directly or through an appropriately qualified outsourced partner) but may also be done by the developer"; a developer shall use a tool qualified by the certification body ([§9.1.3, p. 21](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_Scheme_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=21)). Where the developer generates the evidence, the certification body "shall obtain assurance that all applicable test cases are executed ... in a reliable and repeatable manner, by qualified and independent personnel" ([§6.1(e), p. 14](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_Scheme_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=14)).
- Test outcomes: executed test cases are Pass or Fail; non-executed test cases are Not applicable (profile conditions not met per ICS) or Inconclusive (preconditions not fulfilled; the certification body shall investigate) ([§9.1.4, p. 22](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_Scheme_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=22)).
- The certification decision shall be either Pass or Fail, taken in accordance with the common process of [NL-SYS-01](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-sys-01) ([§6.2, p. 14](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_Scheme_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=14)).
- Certain functional requirements, mainly involving behaviour on the user interface (e.g. information shown by the wallet when asking for user approval during presentation of an attestation), are evaluated via inspection by a human tester; they shall be covered by appropriate test cases complying with this document, and such test cases require test tools ("simulators") to bring the object into the required state ([§9.3, p. 23](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_Scheme_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=23)).
- Certificate validity: the expiry date "shall not be later than 5 years after the issuance date" ([§8.4(q), p. 20](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_Scheme_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=20)).
- The certification body shall ensure the ICS forms referenced in the certificate are publicly available for the entire validity period of the certificate ([§8.4, p. 20](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_Scheme_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=20)).
- Where an evaluation (including surveillance or special evaluations) identifies significant changes to the object of certification, new certification documentation is issued that retains the original expiry date ([§8.4, p. 20](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_Scheme_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=20)).
- Surveillance shall follow the certification cycle of [NL-SYS-01](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-sys-01) and shall take into account the rate of change of the NL-FS.x documents, the referenced technical specifications and the implementing acts ([§8.2, p. 19](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_Scheme_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=19)).
- A surveillance evaluation is triggered at minimum by: (a) a change to the object of certification affecting its functional behaviour or ICS-declared features; (b) a change to implementing acts or referenced technical specifications that materially affects the certified requirements; (c) a change to a test case specification that alters, adds or removes applicable test cases ([§8.2, p. 19](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_Scheme_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=19)).
- Transition arrangements for new or amended requirements or test cases "shall be defined by the certification system" and are not specified in this document ([§8.2, p. 19](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_Scheme_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=19)).
- Recertification is performed against the NL-FS.x documents and test case specifications current at that time; where a component that can be tested individually is unchanged together with its applicable test objectives, its re-evaluation may rely on previous evidence, provided that evidence remains valid and the certification body can verify its continued applicability; new or amended test objectives and changed components are evaluated in full ([§8.3, p. 19](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_Scheme_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=19)).
- Where an NL-FS certificate is suspended, withdrawn or expires, consequences for relying certifications follow from the hierarchy defined in [NL-SYS-01](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-sys-01) and [NL-TL](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-tl) ([§5.3, p. 12](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_Scheme_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=12)).

## Process

Preparatory activities (before any specific certification; the four left-most boxes of the figure) ([§6.1, p. 13–14](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_Scheme_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=13)):
1. (No actor named) The functional requirements are found in the eIDAS Regulation, the implementing acts and referenced technical specifications, and are further identified in [NL-FS-W](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-fs-w) (wallet solutions) or [NL-FS-P](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-fs-p) (PID issuance services) ([§6.1(a), p. 13](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_Scheme_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=13)).
2. Certification body: derives test objectives and test cases (Chapter 7), directly or by reference to an existing test standard such as the FCAF, and develops or references the ICS template(s) ([§6.1(b), p. 13](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_Scheme_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=13)).
3. Certification body (directly or through a qualified outsourced partner): preferably implements the test cases in an automated conformance test tool ([§6.1(c), p. 14](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_Scheme_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=14)).

## ICS (Implementation Conformance Statements)

- ICSs are declarations by the developer about features supported or not supported, consisting of statements answered Yes or No ([§6.3, p. 14](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_Scheme_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=14)).
- Two levels: (1) the ICS in [NL-FS-W](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-fs-w) / [NL-FS-P](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-fs-p), covering the legally mandated features, enabling modularity; (2) technical-specification-level ICSs that come with each test case specification (e.g. ISO/IEC 18013-6, FCAF), covering only optional features of base standards; "Objects of certification are not allowed to pick and choose from mandatory features of these standards"; these are not included in [NL-FS-W](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-fs-w)/[NL-FS-P](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-fs-p) ([§6.3, p. 15](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_Scheme_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=15)).
- Developers shall complete and submit both the [NL-FS-W](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-fs-w)/[NL-FS-P](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-fs-p) ICS and the ICS of each supported technical specification ([§6.3, p. 15](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_Scheme_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=15)).
- A submitted ICS shall at minimum identify the object unambiguously (name, version, build identifier) and declare each statement true or false ([§6.3, p. 16](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_Scheme_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=16)).

## Evidence (§6.4) and certificate content (§8.4)

- Scheme-specific evidence (where the certification body did not run the tests): (a) description of the object incl. build identifier, version, Software Bill of Materials reference and configuration; (b) the [NL-FS-W](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-fs-w)/[NL-FS-P](/edi-stelsel-certification/documents/nl-fs-p) ICS; (c) the technical-specification ICSs; (d) test case specification(s) with versions; (e) the qualified conformance test tool and its version, if applicable; (f) test environment details (e.g. brand, make, OS version of test devices); (g) list of applicable test cases, preferably determined automatically from the ICS; (h) execution reports with verdict per test case, full communication details, and the exact reason for any verdict not Passed; (i) supporting documentation for authenticity and completeness ([§6.4, p. 16](pathname:///files/edi-stelsel/2026-11-06/NL-FS_EUDI_Wallet_Functional_Certification_Scheme_RC1.0_20261106.pdf#page=16)).
