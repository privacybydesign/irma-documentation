The certification system is made up of documents at four levels. <DocLink id="NL-SYS-01" /> shows them in its Figure 3 <Cite doc="NL-SYS-01" page={15}>§4.2</Cite>:

1. **System framework (NL-SYS):** the rules of the whole system, the vocabulary, the organisation of the System Owner, the risk register and the accreditation requirements for CABs.
2. **Top-level certification scheme (NL-TL):** the scheme for the top-level certificate. It combines the results of the subordinate schemes.
3. **Subordinate schemes:** functional certification (NL-FS), cybersecurity certification (NL-CS), and certification of the Wallet Provider (NL-P-W) and the PID Provider (NL-P-P).
4. **Scheme requirements:** the documents with the actual requirements for each subordinate scheme.

## Document identifiers

The release publication uses the identifiers on this site. A few covers use a different reference:

- The Wallet Instance protection profile and its conditions document carry the reference "NL-WI" on their covers; this site calls them NL-CS-WI and NL-CS-WI conditions.
- The WSCA protection profile carries "NL-WSCA", and its conditions document "NL-WSCA-condtions" (as printed); this site calls them NL-CS-WSCA and NL-CS-WSCA conditions.
- NL-CS consists of four parts with the references "NL-CS (part1)" to "NL-CS (part4)".
- NL-P-04P consists of two parts with the references "NL-P-04P (part1)" and "NL-P-04P (part2)".
